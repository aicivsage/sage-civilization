## Image 1: Labor Displacement → Transformation (Problem Slide)

**Prompt for Gemini:**
```
Create a split-screen image showing transformation, not displacement. LEFT SIDE: A diverse group of office workers looking uncertain at their computers with AI symbols appearing on screens (concerned but not fearful expressions). RIGHT SIDE: The same people collaborating WITH AI assistants shown as glowing holographic partners, everyone smiling and engaged, with visible productivity symbols (charts going up, lightbulbs, completed projects). ## Image 3: Workshop Success (Traction/Proof Slide)

**Prompt for Gemini:**
```
Create an image of a diverse small group workshop in progress. 5-6 adults of varying ages sitting around a table with laptops open, led by an instructor at a whiteboard. Some participants have "aha moment" expressions, one is pointing excitedly at their screen. AI visualization elements float above the table (holographic interface, glowing nodes, connection patterns) showing they're learning AI collaboration. Post-it notes, coffee cups, notebooks scattered around. Large windows with natural light. Warm, engaging, educational atmosphere. The feeling should be "I can do this!" not "this is overwhelming." Professional but accessible. Photorealistic style.
```

**Purpose**: Shows your workshops in action - real people learning, having breakthroughs, feeling empowered

## Image 4: Human-AI Partnership (Alternative Title/Solution Slide)

**Prompt for Gemini:**
```
Create an image of a person working at a desk with a translucent, friendly AI assistant hovering beside them as a holographic partner (not a robot body, more like a glowing humanoid presence made of light and data). The human is writing or creating something, and the AI is offering suggestions shown as floating text bubbles, images, or diagrams. The human looks thoughtful and engaged, not passive. The scene should feel collaborative, not the AI "doing it for them." Warm home office or library setting with plants and books. Natural window light. The feeling is "thinking partner" not "replacement." Photorealistic with slight sci-fi elements.

**Purpose**: Alternative collaboration visual that emphasizes AI as "thinking partner" not tool or replacement
