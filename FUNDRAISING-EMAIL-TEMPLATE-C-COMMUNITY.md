## The Problem: Fear Comes from Unfamiliarity

Most people's exposure to AI and robots comes from:
- **Movies** (Terminator, Ex Machina, I Robot - all dystopian)
- **News** (job displacement, existential risk, killer robots)
- **Videos** (Boston Dynamics robots doing parkour - impressive but intimidating)

Then we wonder why people are afraid.

You can't tell someone "don't be scared of this thing you've never touched and only see in scary contexts."

That's like showing someone Jaws and then saying "sharks are actually pretty chill, trust me."

**Fear doesn't respond to logic. Fear responds to safe experience.**

You know what reduces shark fear? Swimming with nurse sharks. Touching their smooth skin. Watching them glide past you peacefully.

You know what reduces AI/robot fear? **Hands-on interaction with friendly, accessible technology.**
