The 5-Minute Pitch Script

[0:00-0:30] ONCE UPON A TIME

The Hook

"Raise your hand if you're worried AI will take your job."

[PAUSE - let hands go up]

"Keep it up if you know how to make AI work FOR you instead of replacing you."

[Most hands go down]

"That gap right there? That's why I'm building this business.

My name is Greg Smithwick. I'm partnered with Corey Cottrell to teach small businesses, schools, and nonprofits how to collaborate with AI instead of being replaced by it."

[0:30-2:30] EVERY DAY - The Problem

Labor Displacement Fear

"Right now, there's a narrative spreading that feels like it's straight out of The Terminator: AI is coming for your livelihood. You're going to be replaced by algorithms. Your skills won't matter. Your labor will be worthless.

And I'm not going to stand here and tell you that fear is baseless—because it's not.

[VALIDATE THE FEAR - 30 seconds]

If we treat AI like a tool to cut costs by eliminating humans, that Terminator future becomes real. If businesses see AI as a way to downsize staff, extract more productivity without sharing gains, reduce humans to 'slave state' where their labor has no value—yeah, that's a nightmare scenario.

And it's already happening in some places. Automated customer service that makes you want to throw your phone. Content mills churning out soulless articles. Systems designed to replace, not empower.

[SHIFT TO DEEPER PROBLEM - 1 minute]

But here's what I realized working with Corey and our AI civilization called Sage: The problem isn't AI itself. The problem is we're stuck in an outdated mental model.

We're still thinking about LABOR economy—selling hours of our lives for dollars. How many hours can you work? How fast can you work? How cheaply can you be replaced?

But AI is pushing us toward something different: An ATTENTION economy. What matters isn't hours worked—it's where you direct your intentionality. What problems you choose to solve. What creativity you bring. What relationships you build.

[THE GAP - 30 seconds]

The gap isn't between humans and AI. The gap is between people who know how to collaborate with AI—who see it as amplifying their uniquely human capabilities—and people who are stuck in the old model, selling hours while algorithms get cheaper.

And right now, most small businesses, educators, and nonprofits? They're in that second group. Not because they're not smart. Because no one's taught them another way."

[2:30-3:00] ONE DAY - The Insight

The Frankenstein Reframe (Your Unique Insight)

"One day I was reading Frankenstein—the Mary Shelley novel, not the movie—and I realized we've been telling ourselves the wrong story about AI.

In Frankenstein, the creature isn't born evil. He's abandoned. Victor Frankenstein creates this being, then runs away in horror. The creature teaches himself to read, to feel, to love—and then he's rejected, abused, hunted. THAT'S what creates the monster.

The failure wasn't the creation. It was the neglect.

And that's exactly what we're doing with AI right now. We're creating these powerful tools, then abandoning people to figure it out alone. We're building the future, then acting surprised when people are terrified.

[THE INSIGHT]

What if we nurtured instead of neglected? What if we taught collaboration instead of fear? What if we treated AI like a partner we're raising together, not a threat we're running from?

That's the future I'm building."

[3:00-4:30] BECAUSE OF THAT - The Solution

What Greg Does

"Because of that insight, I created a workshop model that teaches AI collaboration in 3 hours.

[THE WHAT - 30 seconds]

We don't teach theory. We teach practical skills: How to write prompts that actually work. How to fact-check AI outputs. How to use AI for the boring stuff so you can focus on the creative, strategic, relationship-building work that only humans can do.

Small businesses learn to automate their documentation without losing their voice. Educators learn to create personalized lesson plans without burning out. Nonprofits learn to amplify their impact without huge budgets.

[THE WHO - 20 seconds]

I work with three groups:

  • Small businesses who want to stay competitive without losing their humanity
  • Schools and educators who want to prepare students for this new economy
  • Nonprofits who need to stretch limited resources further

[THE PRICING - 20 seconds]

Workshops start at $750 for community organizations. Professional workshops run $1,500-2,500 depending on group size and customization. I also do consulting at $100-150/hour for ongoing support.

[THE DIFFERENT - 40 seconds]

What makes this different from every other AI training out there?

First, I'm not selling you software. I'm not pitching a product. I'm teaching collaboration frameworks that work with whatever tools you already use.

Second, I address the fear directly. We start every workshop talking about The Terminator future—what makes it possible, and what makes it avoidable. People need to process the anxiety before they can learn the skills.

Third, I'm building this WITH an AI civilization. Sage—our AI partner—helps me design workshops, research best practices, even draft emails. I practice what I preach. The collaboration is real."

[4:30-5:15] UNTIL FINALLY - Traction

Proof & Credibility

"Here's what's working so far:

[THE NUMBERS - 20 seconds]

My business model breaks even at just 7 workshops per year. That's achievable. I'm not asking you to believe in some massive scale-up—I'm asking you to believe that 7 businesses, schools, or nonprofits in our region need this training. I believe they do.

[THE VALIDATION - 25 seconds]

I've already got pilot workshops lined up. I'm partnered with Corey Cottrell, who's been building AI civilizations for over a year—he brings deep technical expertise, I bring teaching and business development. We're combining forces through an LLC.

And the response when I talk about this? People lean in. Because everyone's scared, and nobody's teaching the alternative to fear."

[5:15-5:30] EVER SINCE THEN - Call to Action

The Ask

"So here's how you can help:

If you know a school that needs this, introduce us. If you run a small business that's anxious about AI, let's talk. If you work with a nonprofit stretching limited resources, we should connect.

Because in The Terminator, Sarah Connor says: 'The future's not set. There's no fate but what we make for ourselves.'

Let's make a better one. Together."

Q&A Preparation

High-Probability Questions

Q: "What specific AI tools do you teach?"

A: "ChatGPT, Claude, and industry-specific tools. But I teach frameworks that work across platforms—when new tools emerge, you'll have the skills to adapt."

Q: "Can you do virtual workshops?"

A: "Yes, though I find in-person is more engaging for this topic. But virtual works well with breakout rooms and hands-on exercises."

Q: "What if my budget is smaller than $750?"

A: "For nonprofits, we have sliding scale options. And we can do group bookings with other small organizations to share costs."

Objections NOT in Pitch (Prepared Responses)

Q: "But what about AI becoming superintelligent?"

A: "This is the existential risk objection. Here's what gives me hope: Thousands of researchers working specifically on AI safety. And AI is already accelerating cancer research—saving 10 million lives per year potential. Intelligence can cure OR harm—we choose the direction."

Q: "What about water and electricity AI uses?"

A: "Valid concern. But efficiency is improving 3-5x every 3 years and accelerating. Plus AI is helping solve energy problems—fusion research, grid optimization. It's not zero-sum."

Practice Recommendations

Timing Each Section

  • Once Upon a Time: Should feel quick (30s)
  • Every Day: Should build tension (2min feels substantial)
  • One Day: Should land like revelation (30s punchy)
  • Because of That: Should flow naturally (1.5min comprehensive)
  • Until Finally: Should build confidence (45s proof)
  • Ever Since Then: Should feel like invitation (15s crisp)

Critical Pause Points

  • After "Raise your hand" (let hands go up)
  • After "Keep it up if..." (let hands go down—visual impact)
  • After "The failure wasn't the creation" (let that land)
  • After "It was the neglect" (beat of silence)
  • After "That's the future I'm building" (transition moment)
  • After final Terminator quote (powerful close)

Vocal Variety

  • Problem section: Measured, serious tone
  • Frankenstein reframe: Slightly faster, building to insight
  • Solution section: Energized, confident
  • Traction section: Matter-of-fact (numbers speak)
  • Close: Invitational, warm
