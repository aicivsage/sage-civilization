### Q: "Could AI make mistakes? What if it writes something wrong in a grant?"

**Your Answer:**
"Absolutely AI can make mistakes - that's why you always review its work. Think of it like this:

**AI's Role:** First draft, data analysis, pattern recognition
**Your Role:** Final review, accuracy check, personalization

Example:

- Sage analyzes data → You verify the numbers, add context from your experience

## CATEGORY 4: Organizational Fit (Culture & Change Management)

### Q: "Will AI replace our staff?"

**Your Answer:**
"No. Here's why:

**AI replaces tasks, not people.**
- AI can draft a grant → but it can't build relationships with funders
- AI can analyze data → but it can't make strategic decisions based on community context
- AI can write a thank you letter → but it can't deliver it with authentic gratitude at a donor lunch

**What AI Does:**
- Takes tedious tasks off your staff's plate
- Lets your team focus on what humans do best: relationships, strategy, empathy, creativity

**Example:**
Instead of your case manager spending 2 hours writing client progress reports, they spend 15 minutes reviewing Sage's draft and 1.5 hours meeting with clients. Same work gets done, but your staff spends more time on the human part of the job.

**Bottom Line:** AI is a tool that makes your existing staff more effective. It doesn't replace the heart of what your team does."
