## 💡 Key Findings

### 1. Recommended Structure: Multi-Member LLC

**Why LLC:**
- 1% Greg / 99% Corey ownership minimizes Greg's income
- Operating agreement can hard-cap distributions to Greg
- Business expenses (phone, laptop, vehicle) don't count as Greg's income
- Florida has NO state income tax (huge advantage)
- Low cost: $125 formation, $138.75/year
- Future flexibility to change structure

**Critical Operating Agreement Provisions:**
- Income limitation caps for Greg
- Override rights (Greg can decline distributions)
- Corey priority distributions when Greg can't accept
- Benefit protection acknowledgment

### 2. The $3,000 Mystery Solved

**Finding**: The $3,000/year income limit does NOT match federal SSDI rules.

**Federal SSDI uses monthly limits:**
- ~$1,550/month SGA threshold
- Annual equivalent: ~$18,600/year

**Actual source**: Almost certainly Greg's **Section 8 housing voucher**

**Why this matters:**
- Section 8 has much stricter earned income limits than SSDI
- Section 8 rules may allow business expense deductions
- Section 8 caseworker (not SSA) will be critical contact
- Business structure choice affects voucher eligibility

### 3. Florida Advantages

✅ **No state income tax** (saves thousands vs other states)
✅ **Low LLC formation costs** ($125 vs $500+ elsewhere)
✅ **Simple annual compliance** ($138.75 annual report)
✅ **Strong asset protection** for LLCs
✅ **Quick processing** (1 week online filing)

### 4. Zero-Budget Approach

**Greg has no funds for attorneys/CPAs right now.**

**Phase 1 (Free - Greg can do):**
- Review Section 8 paperwork for income rules
- Call Section 8 caseworker about self-employment income
- Review SSDI award letter for work incentives
- Research "Section 8 self-employment income" online

**Phase 2 (When ready - $125):**
- Form Florida LLC online (fairly simple process)
- Use operating agreement templates (not ideal, but workable)
- MUST understand Section 8 rules first

**Phase 3 (Future - when business has revenue):**
