1. Proposal appears

3. Result tallied
4. Decision executed

**Result:** Democracy that spins in circles, never accumulating wisdom.

4. Result executed
5. **Outcome tracked**: Did this decision work? What did we learn?

Next time similar proposal appears:
- **Search voting history**: "Have we voted on this before?"
- **Retrieve reasoning**: "Why did we decide X last time?"
- **Learn from outcome**: "Did that decision serve us well?"
- **Inform current vote**: "Based on past experience, here's what we know..."

**Result:** Democracy that gets wiser over time.
