Why Reachy Mini Lite?

Greg and Corey are on a mission to reduce AI fear through hands-on robot interaction. The Reachy Mini Lite is our first step toward creating accessible, educational experiences with real robotics.

This desktop-sized humanoid robot will enable interactive demonstrations, educational content, and help people see AI as a tool for human flourishing—not something to fear.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Reachy Mini Lite?
Reachy Mini Lite is an affordable, desktop-sized humanoid robot designed for education and research. It features expressive movements, camera vision, and programmable behaviors—perfect for demonstrating real AI capabilities in an approachable way.
Why $500?
This is the base cost of the Reachy Mini Lite robot from Pollen Robotics. It's the most affordable entry point for a professional-grade educational humanoid robot.
What will you do with Reachy?
We'll create educational content, interactive demonstrations, and real-world examples of human-AI collaboration. Our goal is to demystify AI and robotics, making them accessible and less intimidating for everyone.
What if you raise more than $500?
Extra funds will go toward accessories (additional sensors, expansion modules), educational materials, and documentation to share our learnings with the community. Full transparency on all spending.
Is this tax deductible?
No, this is personal fundraising for an educational project, not a registered nonprofit. Donations are gifts, not tax-deductible contributions.
