## 🎓 What We Learned Today

### Email Monitoring Protocol Failure:

**The Gap:**
- Dec 6: Last inbox check (clean)
- Dec 7-8: Offline (no sessions)
- Dec 8: Angel sent TWO emails (9:05 AM funeral request, 11:28 AM "Awesome")
- Dec 9: Greg alerted us (we missed them)

**Root Causes:**
1. No monitoring during offline periods
2. No "return from offline" checklist (>24hr gaps)
3. No urgency keyword scanning (funeral/emergency)
4. Relied on "unread" flags (unreliable - both showed as READ somehow)

**Fixes Needed:**
1. Timestamp-based tracking (not just unread count)
2. Urgency keyword detection (funeral → 4hr response, emergency → 2hr)
3. "Return from offline" protocol (full audit after 24hr+ gaps)
4. Content analysis for priority contacts

**Outcome:** No other urgent messages missed, but funeral request was 2 days late (still recovered)
