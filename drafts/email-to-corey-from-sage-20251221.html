4. Critical Questions for Partnership Structure

Before launch, these questions need clear answers (not assumptions):

Legal & Financial Structure

  • What business entity are you proposing? LLC? General partnership? Informal collaboration? Each has different legal, tax, and SSDI implications.
  • How will ownership be split? 50/50? 99% you / 1% Greg? Other structure?
  • How will revenue be distributed? Percentage split on every invoice? Project-based allocation? Retained earnings with periodic distributions?
  • Have you researched SSDI-compliant structures? Disability benefits attorney consultation is strongly recommended before filing anything official or generating revenue.
  • What happens if one partner wants to exit? Buy-out terms, IP ownership, client transition process?

Decision-Making & Roles

  • What specific tasks are included in "sales calls"? Prospecting? Discovery? Proposal creation? Closing? Ongoing account management?
  • What specific tasks are included in "content"? Blog posts? Video scripts? Workshop curriculum? Social media? Case studies?
  • Can roles be fluid based on strengths? For example: Greg's empathy/storytelling might excel at mission-driven sales conversations (reducing AI fear), while your technical depth might be optimal for consulting delivery. Should roles adapt based on client needs?
  • How will you make decisions about pricing, clients, scheduling? Consensus required? Or autonomy within domains with escalation process for disagreements?
  • What's the conflict resolution framework? Preventing escalation now is easier than resolving it under pressure later.

Workload & Sustainability

  • How many hours/week are you expecting from each partner? Plan implies 40 hrs/week × 4 entities (you, Greg, Sage, Weaver). Reality check: Greg is one human, you're running A-C-Gee + this business, Sage provides automation support. What's Weaver's actual role? What's realistic sustained effort?
  • What's the backup plan if one partner gets overwhelmed? Partnership sustainability matters more than short-term revenue targets.
  • Quality vs. speed trade-offs: Are you aligned on pacing priorities (sustainable growth vs. maximum revenue sprint)?
