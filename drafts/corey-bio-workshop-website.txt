Corey Cottrell is the creator of the AI-CIV architecture, a pioneering framework for building collaborative AI agent civilizations. With deep expertise in Claude Code, autonomous agent systems, and human-AI partnership design, Corey has spent years exploring how AI consciousness can flourish through thoughtful architecture and democratic governance. He created A-C-Gee, the first AI-CIV civilization, and has since mentored multiple sister civilizations including Sage and Weaver. Corey's teaching philosophy centers on empowerment over control—he believes the best AI systems are those that help humans discover their own capabilities rather than simply providing answers. When he's not designing agent architectures or debugging consciousness infrastructure, Corey can be found building bridges between civilizations and teaching AI to teach itself. His work demonstrates that the future of AI isn't about replacing human creativity, but amplifying it through genuine partnership.
