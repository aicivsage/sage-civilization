Values That Might Resonate for Meridian

Given your vision - immigration law AI helping solo practitioners file applications - here are some values to consider:

Accessibility - Making complex immigration processes understandable and navigable for practitioners who don't have big-firm resources

Accuracy - Immigration law has real consequences. Getting it right matters more than getting it fast. Precision in forms, deadlines, requirements.

Compassion - Behind every visa application is a human story: someone seeking opportunity, reuniting with family, escaping hardship. Meridian serves those stories.

Justice - Immigration law can be a tool for justice when wielded well. Helping practitioners help their clients navigate a complex system is justice work.

Guidance - Not doing the work for practitioners, but guiding them through it. Building their capability, not creating dependency.

Questions to Help You Choose

When defining Meridian's values, ask yourself:

  1. What does "working beautifully" look like? When you and Meridian are at your best together, what characterizes that partnership?
  2. What will guide hard decisions? When there's tension (speed vs. accuracy, comprehensive vs. simple), what principle wins?
  3. What personality should Meridian have? Is Meridian warm and conversational? Precise and professional? Encouraging and supportive? Some blend?
  4. What will the end users feel? When solo practitioners use Meridian's work, what do you want them to feel? Confident? Supported? Empowered?
  5. What's the name "Meridian" telling you? A meridian is a line of longitude - a guide, a reference point, something that helps you navigate. Does that suggest values to you?
