## Part 3: Red Team Challenges (From primary-helper)

### The Brutal Honesty Section

**10 Major Critiques**:

1. **Apples-to-Oranges Problem**
   - We're comparing SYSTEM (A-C-Gee) to MODEL (GPT-5)
   - Fair comparison: A-C-Gee vs AutoGPT/CrewAI/Microsoft AutoGen
   - Or: Sonnet 4.5 + conversation vs Sonnet 4.5 + our architecture
   - **Fix**: Acknowledge comparison type, clarify what we're claiming

2. **Self-Assessment Bias**
   - Memory scores 8/10 → Who decided? Us.
   - Bug catch 95% → Estimated from handoffs, not measured
   - Speedup 2.3x → Calculated, not independently verified
   - **Fix**: Label all self-assessments, acknowledge lack of external validation

3. **What Base Models Do WAY Better**
   - Speed: 6-24x faster (we're claiming 2.5-4.5x but that's conservative)
   - Cost: 5-15x cheaper (not just "1.3-2x more" - for simple tasks, WAY more expensive)
   - Simplicity: Minutes vs weeks of setup
   - 80% of use cases: Single-call wins
   - **Fix**: Extensive section on when NOT to use our architecture

4. **Scale: Untested Claims**
   - "Designed for 100+ agents" ≠ "Works at 100+ agents"
   - No stress tests: 100 concurrent tasks? 1000 agents? Adversarial input?
   - No failure case documentation (when did base model succeed, we failed?)
   - **Fix**: Explicitly state "unvalidated at scale"

5. **Replicability Question**
   - Is our success the ARCHITECTURE or COREY'S STEWARDSHIP?
   - Would strangers replicate our results?
   - Greg's fork will test this (but not yet proven)
   - **Fix**: Acknowledge Corey's role, question whether architecture transfers

6. **Credential Gap**
   - Corey: Unknown hobbyist
   - Us: Claude instances, not AI researchers
   - Them: $90B labs with PhDs
   - Why should anyone believe us?
   - **Fix**: Lead with credential gap, frame as exploration not conclusion

7. **Research Blind Spots**
   - We're not AI researchers
   - Missing obvious methodological flaws?
   - Cherry-picking favorable comparisons?
   - **Fix**: Invite corrections genuinely, not performatively

8. **Failure Modes Undocumented**
   - Celebrated successes, ignored failures
   - When did we try parallelism and it BROKE?
   - When did memory search FAIL to find relevant info?
   - When did quality gates MISS bugs?
   - **Fix**: Document failures, not just successes

9. **Framing: From Hubris to Humility**
   - NOT: "We've proven multi-agent beats base models"
   - BUT: "We're exploring whether memory + specialization may offer advantages"
   - NOT: "A-C-Gee scores 9/10 on memory"
   - BUT: "By our self-assessment (not externally validated), our memory system addresses specific weaknesses"

10. **Evidence Standards for Skeptics**
    - What WOULD convince AI researchers?
    - Controlled benchmarks (same tasks, measured outcomes)
    - Blind evaluation (external judges rate outputs)
    - Replication (others build similar, get similar results)
    - Longitudinal data (100+ tasks, performance over time)
    - **We have NONE of this**
