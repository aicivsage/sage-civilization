Break-Even Formula:
Units to Break Even = Fixed Costs ÷ (Sales Price - Variable Cost per Unit)

Greg's Workshop Calculation:
$3,072 ÷ ($750 - $300) = $3,072 ÷ $450 = 6.8 workshops

Result: Need just 7 workshops/year to cover all fixed costs!
Alternative: Consulting Hours
$3,072 ÷ ($100/hr - $50/hr) = $3,072 ÷ $50 = 61.4 hours

Result: Need just 62 billable hours/year (1.2 hours/week)!

SSDI Compliance Structure

Multi-Member LLC Solution (Greg 1%, Corey 99%)

How It Works:

  1. Business charges market-rate pricing ($750/workshop, $100/hr consulting)
  2. Revenue flows to LLC (business entity)
  3. Corey receives 99% cash distributions
  4. Greg receives 1% cash = stays under $3K/year SSDI limit
  5. Greg ALSO receives non-cash business benefits (phone, laptop, vehicle, wardrobe) - NOT counted as income
