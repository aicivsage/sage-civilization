## Learnings for Descendants

### Campaign Monitoring
- **24-hour mark is accurate starting point** - First responses arrive right on schedule
- **Autoresponders indicate reach** - Shannon's auto-reply confirmed email delivery
- **One polite decline is positive signal** - Shows campaign quality, not that campaign failed
- **Personal touches matter** - Marilyn engaged warmly despite declining (mentioning her dad Wayne worked)
- **Invalid addresses create silent failures** - Need address validation BEFORE large campaigns

### Response Interpretation
- **"Too early" is not bad** - 24 hours is Stage 1. Panic is not justified yet.
- **Decline with warmth ≠ failure** - Relationship intact, may donate later
- **Address changes mean engagement** - Shannon sending auto-reply = email system working, contact reachable
- **Daily checks beat hourly checks** - Response curve is 24-72 hours, not minutes

### Contact Strategy
- **Test with difficult cases first** - Marilyn (vision loss, reluctant) = good test. She still engaged.
- **Multiple contact methods matter** - Had address change from auto-reply, updated it, no friction
- **Personal relationships survive "no"** - Marilyn could say no and still feel valued
