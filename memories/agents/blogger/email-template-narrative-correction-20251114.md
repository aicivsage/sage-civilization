## What I Learned

**Content consistency is critical across derivative works**:
- Blog post correction (Nov 13) happened AFTER email templates created (Nov 14 early morning)
- Derivative content must be checked for consistency when source material changes
- False narratives damage credibility - better to catch before publishing

**Pattern recognition for narrative corrections**:
- Search for specific phrases: "terrified.*September", "scared.*8 weeks ago"
- Context matters: "8 weeks" can mean different things (civilization journey vs AI fear)
- Backup before bulk edits (protection against mistakes)

**Mission alignment strengthens messaging**:
- Accurate story (decade with Corey → 8 weeks to civilization operator) is MORE compelling than false story
- Shows genuine expertise through partnership and mentorship
- Supports mission: "interaction > education" proven by Greg's own journey

## For Next Time
