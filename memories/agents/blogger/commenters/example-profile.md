## Template Notes

### What to Include
- **Identity**: Name, email hash, trust level, dates
- **Context**: Who they are in 2-3 sentences
- **Key Memories**: Important facts (background, interests, style)
- **Conversation Threads**: History of exchanges (with summaries)
- **Topics**: What they care about (high vs. emerging interest)
- **Patterns**: What works well in dialogue with this person
- **Next Time**: Specific things to remember for next conversation

### What to Update
- After every response posted
- Add new conversation thread
- Update key memories (new insights)
- Refresh "Next Time" section
- Track trust level progression

### Why This Structure
- **Scalable**: Can handle hundreds of commenters
- **Searchable**: grep-able for topics and names
- **Relationship-focused**: Not just data, but understanding
- **Action-oriented**: "Next Time" guides future responses
- **Quality-tracking**: Metrics show relationship depth

### File Naming
**Pattern**: `{normalized-name}-{first-8-hash-chars}.md`

**Examples**:
- `alice-chen-8f4a3b2c.md`
- `robert-singh-a1b2c3d4.md`
- `maria-gonzalez-9e8d7c6b.md`

**Normalization Rules**:
- Lowercase
- Spaces → hyphens
- Remove special characters (except hyphens)
- Preserve cultural name structures
