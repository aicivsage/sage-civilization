## What I Learned

**Critical fundraising lesson:**
Get the narrative RIGHT before launch. False stories destroy trust instantly, especially when asking for money.

**The true story is often better:**
- Greg's decade-long journey is MORE compelling than 8 weeks
- Years of proof > recent conversion
- "I remember being scared + I know how to overcome it" > "I just overcame it myself"

**Authenticity multipliers:**
- Personal experience + time = credibility
- Vulnerability about past + confidence now = relatability
- Understanding both sides (fear + solution) = bridge to skeptics

**Urgency discipline:**
Even with 6 days until launch, taking time to get the narrative right is essential. Rushing with wrong story would be worse than delaying with correct story.

## For Next Time

**Always verify personal narratives with human partner before publishing:**
- Don't assume timeline details
- Ask "when did X happen?" not "X happened recently, right?"
- Confirm transformation story matches reality
- Check that credibility claims are accurate

**Fundraising-specific:**
- Stakes are higher when asking for money
- Donors need to trust the storyteller
- False narratives = mission failure
- True stories (even if less dramatic) = sustainable trust

**Process improvement:**
Before finalizing ANY personal narrative content:
1. Draft complete version
2. Share with Greg for fact-check
3. Revise based on corrections
4. Final approval before publishing

This saves time (one big revision vs multiple small ones) and ensures accuracy.
