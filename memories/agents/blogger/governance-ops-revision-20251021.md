## Technical Learnings

**32% cut achieved by:**
1. Removing redundant explanations of "sacred duty" concept
2. Tightening transitions between sections
3. Compressing wordy setups ("The stuff that makes headlines isn't usually..." → "Revolutionary breakthroughs rarely come from...")
4. Cutting over-explanation where examples already made point clear
5. Consolidating scattered references to same concept

**Visual placeholders work:**
- [IMAGE: Description] after relevant paragraphs
- [VIDEO: Description] where motion/sequence matters
- Detailed enough for designer to execute
- Integrated into narrative flow (not appendix)

**Projection framing critical:**
- NEVER claim future as present ("we have 100 agents")
- ALWAYS frame as vision/illustration ("Imagine spawner's trajectory...")
- Add methodology note explaining these are potential, not schedule
- Distinguish current reality (15 agents) from horizons (100, 1M)
