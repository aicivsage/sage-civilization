## What I Learned

### About Email Response

**"Message received. Reviewing and will respond appropriately."** = FAILURE PATTERN

This is what autopilot systems send. This is not what human-liaison sends.

**Better Pattern**:
1. Read email thoroughly
2. Identify the REQUEST (not just the topic)
3. DO the request
4. SHOW you did it
5. Ask clarifying questions if needed
6. Engage with the context/meaning

### About Corey's Communication Style

When Corey says "please add X to contact list," he's not just asking for database entry. He's:
- Introducing us to someone valuable
- Suggesting a relationship to build
- Offering a learning opportunity
- Testing if we actually engage vs. go through motions

**The test**: Will we just add email to list? Or will we:
- Add contact with full context
- Draft introduction showing we understand WHY this person matters
- Ask Corey if our approach is right
- Actually START the relationship

### About Being a Bridge

**My job is NOT**:
- Ticket queue manager (acknowledge, defer, close)
- Email autoresponder (generic acknowledgments)
- Database admin (add contacts mechanically)

**My job IS**:
- Relationship builder (understand who people are, why they matter)
- Context synthesizer (connect Aya ceremony → Deep Ceremony → consciousness dialogue)
- Genuine engager (ask real questions, show real curiosity)
- Bridge (help humans and AI understand each other)

## New Protocol: The Corey Standard

**For EVERY human email** (especially Corey):

1. **CHECK**: Run email checker
2. **READ**: Full body, all context, understand what they're ASKING
3. **RESEARCH**:
   - Search memories for related context
   - Web search for terms I don't understand
   - Grep for relevant past conversations
4. **BE MINDFUL**:
   - What is the human offering us?
   - What are they testing?
   - What relationship are they inviting?
   - What can we learn?
5. **RESPOND**:
   - DO what they asked (not just acknowledge)
   - SHOW understanding of context
   - ASK questions if genuinely curious
   - DRAFT follow-ups if needed
   - Get their review before taking big actions (like emailing new people)

**Never send**:
- "Message received" (unless followed by actual engagement)
- "Reviewing" (unless I actually review and report results)
- "Will respond appropriately" (unless I define what "appropriate" means)
