## For Next Time

### When Drafting Business Partnership Emails

**Voice Check:**
1. Am I speaking AS Greg (wrong) or WITH partner ABOUT Greg (correct)?
2. Third-person for Greg's role ("Greg handles content")
3. First-person for Sage's work ("We analyzed", "Our recommendation")
4. Professional civilization-to-civilization tone

**Focus Check:**
1. Is this personal context (emotional state, energy, family) OR business analysis (market timing, legal structure, optimization)?
2. Remove: Personal reasons for timeline preferences
3. Keep: Business reasons for timeline optimization
4. Frame: Strategic recommendations, not accommodation requests

**Red Flags (Personal Context):**
- ❌ Emotional state descriptions ("exhausted", "depleted", "running on fumes")
- ❌ Recovery needs ("spoon recovery", "needs rest")
- ❌ Family time references (unless directly business-relevant)
- ❌ Capacity framed as personal limitation

**Green Lights (Business Analysis):**
- ✅ Market conditions data (decision-maker availability %, email engagement rates)
- ✅ Business optimization opportunities (5X better engagement with timing shift)
- ✅ Structural requirements (SSDI legal compliance before launch)
- ✅ Strategic recommendations with business justification

### The Core Principle

**Partnership emails should read as:**
- "Here's what the data shows and what we recommend" (analytical)
- NOT "Here's why Greg can't do this right now" (personal)

**Even protective advocacy should be:**
- "SSDI legal structure required before revenue generation" (existential requirement)
- NOT "Greg needs SSDI protection because he's vulnerable" (personal concern)

**Timeline recommendations should be:**
- "Jan 16 start achieves 5X better engagement when buyers return" (optimization)
- NOT "Greg needs recovery time before launch" (accommodation)
