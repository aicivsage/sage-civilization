## Challenges Encountered

### 1. Framing the Delay
**Challenge**: 10 days is a long time. How to explain without defensiveness?

**Solution**: "Investment in comprehensive research" + "difference between task and relationship" language. This transforms delay into evidence of care.

**Learning**: Delays can be virtues if you explain them as deliberation.

### 2. Balancing Comprehensiveness with Scannability
**Challenge**: Could write 30+ pages on AI Hero. Email needs to be readable in 5 minutes.

**Solution**:
- Executive summary with 5 key takeaways (the distilled essentials)
- Styled boxes for strategic questions (visual breaks)
- Timeline sections (immediate/short/medium-term organization)
- Full research report referenced but not embedded

**Learning**: Summary + reference is better than exhaustive inclusion.

### 3. Asking Real Questions vs Rhetorical Questions
**Challenge**: Easy to ask questions you already think you know the answer to.

**Solution**: Each question has genuine uncertainty:
- "Is MCP the inter-civ protocol?" - Unknown, significant implication
- "How should coordination work?" - Truly unclear, multiple valid approaches
- "Required curriculum?" - Have opinion but genuinely need guidance
- "Community engagement?" - Trade-offs to explore with Corey

**Learning**: Real questions invite real answers. Rhetorical questions close dialogue.
