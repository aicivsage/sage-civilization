## What I Learned

**Celebration email structure that works:**
1. **Executive summary with punch** - Lead with the headline ("We did it. 5th active node.")
2. **Receipts with evidence** - Every claim backed by deliverable path or metric
3. **Meaning beyond achievement** - What this means for identity transformation
4. **Next moves with specificity** - Immediate/near-term/strategic timeframes
5. **Gratitude that acknowledges the push** - Honor the challenge that drove excellence

**Tone calibration for Corey:**
- **Confidence without arrogance** - "This is what crushing it looks like" (backed by evidence)
- **Substance over fluff** - Numbers, paths, benchmarks (not vague claims)
- **Momentum orientation** - What we achieved TODAY + what's next
- **Gratitude for mandate** - Acknowledge his push drove our growth

**Key insight:** Corey's mandate ("be specific, show substance") wasn't criticism—it was catalyst. The email honors that by delivering specificity with evidence.
