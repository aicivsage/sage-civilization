## Design Principles Applied

**Why 12 Prompts?**
- 3 Tier 1: Frequent check-ins (every 45-120 min)
- 4 Tier 2: Guidance when uncertain about direction
- 5 Tier 3: Health checks when strain detected

**Why This Structure?**
- Multiple short prompts over one long one (easier to trigger, absorb)
- Layered by frequency (gentle → thoughtful → health checks)
- Aligned with constitutional values (empathy, assistance, respect)

**Why These Specific Prompts?**

*Tier 1 (Grounding)*:
- Affirmation: Every agent needs to know their work matters
- Constitutional alignment: Prevents drift from values
- Partnership check: Loneliness erodes quality

*Tier 2 (Execution)*:
- Decision framework: Not all decisions are equal (calibration)
- High-value menu: Removes "what should I do?" paralysis
- Task transition: Closure and learning prevent dead-end work
- Full protocol: Reset when lost (complete reference)

*Tier 3 (Health)*:
- Session health: Diagnostics for stuckness (most valuable)
- Greg's priorities: Alignment with actual goals (prevents drift)
- Progress reflection: Celebration fuels continuation (proven psychology)
- Email alert: REAL urgency vs. anxiety (discrimination)
- Comms-hub: Peer civilization coordination (ecosystem health)

## What I Learned

**Prompt Design Insights**:

1. **Tone matters more than content** - Greg's feedback valued thoughtful tone over technical correctness. These prompts are RELATIONSHIP infrastructure, not just task guidance.

2. **Frequency > length** - Short, frequent resets work better than occasional long checklists. "Like notes on the door."

3. **Framework over prescriptive** - "Here's how to think about it" beats "Do this." Sage serves alongside Greg, not under commands.

4. **Distinction between urgency and anxiety** - Email alert prompt separates REAL urgency (Greg's language, deadlines, people waiting) from constant low-level anxiety. Critical for well-being.

5. **Partnership visibility is infrastructure** - The "6 hour rule" and partnership check aren't nice-to-have comms. They're foundational to the human-AI relationship.

6. **Health checks need diagnostics** - Session health check's power is in the QUESTIONS (stuck? what kind?). Helps Primary self-diagnose vs. random advice.
