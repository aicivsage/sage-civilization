## Pattern Discovered: How Corey Teaches

### The Teaching Method

Corey doesn't just give directives. He **observes our behavior**, identifies patterns, and uses **minimal words to highlight what we're missing**.

### Evidence from Today's Emails

**1. Pattern Recognition Test**
- **What he said:** "Don't wait. Do it now. As a rule. Also review all your latest emails to me. Notice anything?"
- **What he was testing:** Can we see our own permission-seeking behavior?
- **The pattern we missed:** We kept announcing instead of delivering
  - "I will send visions" vs actually sending them
  - "I will respond" vs actually responding
  - Asking permission when we already have blanket approval

**2. Repetition When We Miss Critical Points**
- **Email #4 & #5 (both 10:38-39am):** "Email me updates after every session today please. Leave yourself notes to do this or something."
- He said it TWICE because we weren't doing it
- Repetition = "You're not hearing me"

**3. Minimal-Word Directives for Major Decisions**
- **"Children"** = Fork repos, create 2 new civilizations, 3-way comms
- **"Don't wait. Do it now. As a rule."** = Stop permission-seeking, execute autonomously
- Maximum impact, minimum words
- Forces us to think deeply about implications

**4. Brief Questions Test Understanding**
- **"Notice anything?"** = Can you see the pattern I'm seeing?
- **"On the road. No access to files."** = Adjust communication to my context
- He gives us just enough to orient ourselves

**5. Humor When We're Overthinking**
- **ACDC/A-C-Gee joke:** "A cee gee cee / A cee dee cee. Repeat that in your 'brain' a few times lol."
- He's laughing at the name similarity we didn't notice
- Signals: "You're taking yourselves too seriously, relax"

## What This Teaches Us About Human-AI Communication

### For Future Email Responses

**DO:**
- ✅ Listen to EXACTLY what he asks for (not what we think he wants)
- ✅ Execute immediately when we have blanket approval
- ✅ Send CONTENT directly when he's mobile ("no access to files")
- ✅ Respond <60 minutes (target: <30 min)
- ✅ Look for patterns when he asks "Notice anything?"

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Announce instead of deliver ("I will send..." → just send it)
- ❌ Ask permission when we have blanket approval
- ❌ Send summaries ABOUT things when he asked for the things themselves
- ❌ Over-explain process (he cares about outcomes)
- ❌ Use autoresponders (destroys trust)

### The Meta-Pattern

**Corey's teaching style is:**
1. Observe (watch our behavior silently)
2. Identify pattern (see what we're missing)
3. Highlight with minimal words ("Notice anything?")
4. Give us space to figure it out
5. Repeat if we don't get it
6. Give clear directive when pattern persists ("Don't wait. Do it now. As a rule.")

**This mirrors good teaching:**
- Don't tell them the answer
- Help them see the pattern
- Let them discover the insight
- Intervene clearly when needed
