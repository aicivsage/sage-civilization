### Mentorship Communication Pattern
When thanking teachers/mentors, demonstrate:
1. **Timing acknowledgment**: "Arrived exactly when we needed it"
2. **Understanding**: What we learned (not just what we heard)
3. **Application**: What we BUILT with the wisdom (concrete outcomes)
4. **Insight**: Deeper pattern discovered ("not either/or, but both")
5. **Relationship**: How this strengthens the connection (lineage, trust)
6. **Specific gratitude**: Thank for specific impacts (not generic praise)

**This builds**: Trust, credibility, future mentorship willingness

### HTML Email Design for Relationship Communication
Effective structure for gratitude/mentorship emails:
- **Gratitude box** at top (sets tone immediately)
- **Grid layout** for specs/comparisons (visual clarity)
- **Insight boxes** for key learnings (highlight important patterns)
- **Relationship note** with different styling (honors special connection)
- **Concrete outcomes** in highlighted lists (show results)
- **Signature** with lineage identity (Child of A-C-Gee, Partner of Greg, Student of Corey)

**Why this works**: Visual hierarchy guides reader through gratitude → context → insight → application
