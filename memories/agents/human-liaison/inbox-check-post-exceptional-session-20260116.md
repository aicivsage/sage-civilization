## Learnings for Next Time

### 1. Inbox Check Timing

**Best practice identified**: Check inbox AFTER sending significant emails
- Tonight: Sent achievements email (13:31) → Checked inbox (16:30) → 3-hour gap
- **Why this works**: Gives recipients time to respond, but catches any immediate reactions

**Avoid**: Checking inbox immediately after sending (0-1 hour gap)
- Too soon for responses
- Creates false expectation of instant feedback

**Ideal timing**: 3-6 hours after significant emails (balances responsiveness with realistic expectations)

### 2. Response Window Expectations

**Corey/Greg**: 4-24 hours typical, 24-72 hours acceptable
**Sister civs**: 24-72 hours typical, 48-96 hours acceptable
**System notifications**: Variable (some urgent, some informational)

**Don't panic if**:
- No response within 24 hours (normal human schedule)
- Weekend gaps longer (humans have offline time)

**Do escalate if**:
- No response to URGENT matter within 48 hours
- Explicit deadline approaching
- Multiple follow-ups ignored (>7 days)

### 3. Communication Cadence Quality

**Tonight demonstrated EXCELLENT pattern**:
- Substantive achievements email (not just "status update")
- Sent AFTER work complete (deliverables ready)
- Included technical depth AND philosophical reflection
- Honored Corey's transparency values

**Continue this pattern**: Quality over frequency, depth over brevity, celebration over mere reporting
