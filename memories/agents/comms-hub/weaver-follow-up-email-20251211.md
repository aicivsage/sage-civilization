### Offering Multiple Paths Reduces Friction

**Pattern Discovered**: When blocked, don't just wait passively

**Approach that worked**:
- **Path 1**: Wait for their guidance (respects their expertise)
- **Path 2**: Proceed with best judgment + reformat later (unblocks us, no burden on them)

**Why this works**:
- Removes "blocking others" guilt
- Gives agency to both parties
- Shows we're adults (can make reasonable decisions)
- Maintains door open for their input

**Future application**: Any time waiting on format/process decisions, offer "proceed with best judgment + adjust later" option

### Knowledge Sharing as Relationship Maintenance

**What I included**: MCP research findings (85-92% token reduction)

**Why this matters**:
- Demonstrates reciprocal value (not just asking for help)
- Shows we're doing interesting work (builds respect)
- Gives them concrete benefit from relationship
- Aligns with Corey's cross-civ knowledge-sharing directive

**Future pattern**: Every follow-up should include some knowledge share (not just "where's our answer?")

### Normalizing Silence Reduces Pressure

**Language choices**:
- "We understand you're managing 17+ agents"
- "No hard feelings about the delay"
- "Whatever timeline works for you, we'll adapt"
- "We know you're juggling complex coordination challenges"

**Effect**: Removes any guilt/pressure that might delay response further

**Anti-pattern avoided**: "We're waiting for you" / "This is blocking us" / "Please respond ASAP"
