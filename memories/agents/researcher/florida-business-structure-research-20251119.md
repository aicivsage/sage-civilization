## What I Learned

### Florida LLC Formation is Straightforward

**Key findings**:
- $125 total initial filing ($100 Articles + $25 registered agent)
- $138.75 annual report (due May 1)
- 1 week processing time (online filing)
- Operating agreement not required but strongly recommended
- Registered agent must have Florida street address

### Florida Tax Advantages are Significant

**Critical insight**: Florida's **no state income tax** fundamentally changes entity choice analysis

**Impact on double taxation**:
- C-Corp in Florida: 5.5% FL corporate tax + 0% FL tax on dividends
- C-Corp in California: 8.84% CA corporate + 13.3% CA on dividends
- Florida C-Corp saves ~16 percentage points vs high-tax states

**But LLC still superior for small business**:
- Zero FL state tax on pass-through income (vs 5.5% C-Corp)
- No double taxation
- Simpler compliance

### The $3,000 Limit Mystery

**CRITICAL FINDING**: The referenced "$3,000/year income limit" does NOT match any federal SSDI rule.

**Federal SSDI uses**:
- Monthly Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) limits (~$1,550/month for 2025)
- Annual equivalent would be ~$18,600/year (far higher than $3,000)

**Possible sources of $3,000 limit**:
1. SSI (Supplemental Security Income) rather than SSDI - different program
2. Florida state benefit (Medicaid, housing assistance, cash assistance)
3. Local housing authority specific rule
4. Misunderstanding of Trial Work Period or other SSDI provisions

**This is URGENT** - entire entity choice depends on which benefit has which limit.

### Operating Agreement is Key Protective Mechanism

**Insight**: For SSDI recipient business owner, operating agreement must include:

1. Hard income caps on distributions to disabled member
2. Override rights for disabled member to decline distributions
3. Priority distributions to non-disabled partner when disabled partner cannot accept
4. Benefit protection acknowledgment by all members

**This is NOT standard LLC language** - requires disability benefits expertise.

### Disability Benefits Research is Complex

**Key learning**: Government disability benefit programs are:
- Complex (multiple programs, overlapping rules, state variations)
- Poorly documented online (sites often inaccessible or unclear)
- Highly individualized (depends on specific benefits received)
- Constantly changing (income limits adjust annually)

**Implication**: For SSDI/disability research:
- Always recommend professional consultation
- Never make definitive recommendations without confirming client's specific benefits
- Emphasize urgency of identifying benefit letter details
- Provide frameworks and questions to ask professionals
