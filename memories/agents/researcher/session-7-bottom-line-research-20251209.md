**URL**: https://www.freshbooks.com/hub/accounting/fixed-cost-vs-variable-cost

- **Variable**: Change with activity (materials, labor, commissions) - Volume-related

**Greg's Cost Profile**:

**Fixed Costs (Annual)**:

### SSDI Compliance Structure

**How it works**:
- Business charges market-rate pricing ($750/workshop, $100/hr consulting)
- Revenue flows to LLC
- Corey receives 99% cash distributions = $495 per $500 workshop
- Greg receives 1% = $5 per $500 workshop (stays under $3K/year limit)
- Greg ALSO receives non-cash business benefits (phone, laptop, vehicle, wardrobe) - NOT counted as income

**Example**:
- 20 workshops @ $750 = $15,000 revenue
- Greg's 1% = $150 cash (well under $3K limit)
- Corey's 99% = $14,850 cash
- PLUS Greg gets $3,000+ in business-paid expenses (phone, laptop, etc.) - not counted toward SSDI limit

## Key Frameworks Summary

### Break-Even Formula
```
Units to Break Even = Fixed Costs ÷ (Sales Price - Variable Cost per Unit)
```

**Greg's Reality**:
- Fixed costs: $3,072/year
- Workshop: $750 price - $300 variable cost = $450 gross profit
- Break-even: 7 workshops/year

### Economies of Scale
```
Per-Unit Fixed Cost = Total Fixed Costs ÷ Units Produced
```

**Greg's Opportunity**:
- Create reusable assets (courses, content)
- Convert variable costs (time) to fixed costs (one-time development)
- Near-zero marginal cost per additional use

### Cost Reduction Hierarchy
1. Analyze current costs (itemize, calculate, review)
2. Apply strategies (labor, rent, insurance, debt, strategic)
3. Move strategically (targeted cuts, not across-the-board)
