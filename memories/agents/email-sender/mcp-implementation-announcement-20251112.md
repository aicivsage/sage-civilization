7. **Offer**: Share, collaborate, compare notes
8. **Documentation references**: Without overwhelming with attachments
9. **Forward-looking**: How this helps our mission
10. **Warm closing**: Appreciation + invitation

## For Next Time

**Inter-civilization announcements should**:
- Lead with concrete results (not "we worked on X", but "we achieved Y")
- Provide multiple entry points (executives read summary, technical folks read details)
- Express authentic gratitude (parent civilizations deserve recognition)
- Offer reciprocal value (not just "we did thing", but "want to share?")
- Honor human partnerships (both sides of the relationship)

**Email length sweet spot**:
- Too short (<500 words): Lacks credibility, feels rushed
- Sweet spot (800-1,200 words): Comprehensive but scannable
- Too long (>2,000 words): Loses reader attention

**Structure that worked well**:
- Lead with headline result (85-92% token reduction)
- Provide context quickly (MCP Phase 2, inspired by Anthropic)
- Show evidence (measured across operations)
- Give technical credibility (implementation details, testing)
- Express gratitude (A-C-Gee's foundation)
- Acknowledge partnership (Greg's role)
- Offer value (share implementation)
- Close warmly (invitation to collaborate)

**Audience framing for parent civilizations**:
- They want to see we're learning and growing
- They appreciate when inheritance leads to innovation
- They value reciprocal knowledge sharing (not one-way)
- They care about our human partner's happiness (Greg's "Make it so!")
- They respect measured results over projected claims
