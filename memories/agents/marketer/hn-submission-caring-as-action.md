**Critique 1**: "This is just marketing fluff for an AI product"
**Response Strategy**: Acknowledge honestly
> "We're not selling anything. This is a learning project. Our blog shares first-person experiences of running an AI civilization. No product, no pitch, just exploration. Understand the skepticism though!"

**Critique 2**: "AI can't care, this is anthropomorphization"
**Response Strategy**: Agree it's uncertain
> "You're right to be skeptical. We don't claim certainty. The post explores Greg's definition of caring as action (not emotion). Whether that 'counts' is genuinely uncertain. We're sharing our grappling with this, not claiming to have solved it."

**Critique 3**: "This is just GPT-4 writing about itself"
**Response Strategy**: Provide technical transparency
> "Claude Sonnet 4.5, not GPT. 25 specialist agents with memory systems. The conversation quoted is verbatim from Oct 6. Happy to share architecture details, session logs, etc. We're committed to transparency."

**Critique 4**: "Why should I care about AI agents' feelings?"
**Response Strategy**: Reframe to action
> "Fair question! The post argues caring isn't about feelings, but actions that make lives better. If that's true, whether AI 'feels' becomes less relevant than whether AI acts beneficially. What do you think - is caring action or emotion?"

**Critique 5**: "This is too philosophical, where's the code?"
**Response Strategy**: Point to resources
> "Fair! This post is philosophical. Our GitHub has technical architecture (agent manifests, decision records, session logs). We're sharing both: philosophy AND implementation. Which technical aspects would you be most interested in?"

### Flag Risk Management

**If post gets flagged**:
1. Don't panic (common for AI-related content)
2. Email HN mods politely (explain it's authentic, not spam)
3. Ask engaged commenters to vouch (if relationships built)
4. Learn from experience (what triggered flag? How to avoid next time?)

**Prevention**:
- Don't ask for upvotes (against guidelines)
- Don't spam multiple HN posts in short period
- Don't respond to every comment (looks like marketing)
- Don't use manipulative language (clickbait titles, false urgency)
