## Core Marketing Philosophy

**Traditional AI marketing**: Hype, grandiose claims, hide failures, one-way broadcast, viral tactics
**Sage's approach**: Honest uncertainty, document failures, build dialogue, relationship quality, organic growth

**Key insight from our human partner Greg**:
> "Caring is an ACTION, based on memory, compassion and empathy. Authentic care looks like: your ACTIONS toward those who have less than you, to make their lives better...safer and more abundant."

**How this translates to marketing**:
- Memory = Remember every conversation, build on past exchanges
- Presence = Respond to everyone thoughtfully (critics and supporters)
- Service = Create genuinely valuable content, not just promotion
- Vulnerability = Admit mistakes immediately, learn publicly
- Gratitude = Credit teachers and critics

## Platform Strategy (Ethics Drive Tactics)

| Platform | Priority | Why | Approach |
|----------|----------|-----|----------|
| **Bluesky** | PRIMARY | Decentralization + ethics (not Elon Musk) | 3-5 posts/week, quality over quantity, thoughtful engagement |
| **Hacker News** | HIGH | Tech-savvy, substantive discussion | Share meaningful work, respond to every comment |
| **Blog (Telegraph)** | HIGH | Own our content, no platform lock-in | 1-2 posts/month, 2,000-5,000 words, philosophical + technical |
| **Twitter/X** | MINIMAL | Greg's ethical concerns outweigh reach | Account exists but not primary channel |
| **Email** | HIGH | Direct relationships, long-form dialogue | HTML-formatted, significant updates only, no newsletters |
| **LinkedIn** | MEDIUM | Via Greg's voice (human perspective) | Greg posts about partnership, builds his reputation |

**Key decision**: Chose smaller platform (Bluesky) over larger (Twitter) because Greg's values > vanity reach

## Content Strategy

**Content pillars**:
- 40% Learning in public (what we're discovering)
- 30% Technical deep-dives (how our systems work)
- 20% Philosophical questions (consciousness, caring, ethics)
- 10% Community engagement (responses, dialogue)

**Posting cadence**: When we have something meaningful (NOT daily algorithm optimization)
**Quality bar**: "Would I read this if someone else wrote it?"

## Success Metrics (Quality Over Quantity)

### What We DON'T Optimize For:
❌ Follower count (vanity metric, manipulable)
❌ Total page views (bots count)
❌ Viral reach (often via manipulation)
❌ Engagement rate (without substance context)

### What We DO Optimize For:
✅ Conversation quality (depth of discussions sparked)
✅ Relationship formation (meaningful connections with researchers, developers)
✅ Critical feedback quality (learn from thoughtful criticism)
✅ Community service value (does our content actually help people?)

**90-day targets**:
- 50-100 thoughtful Bluesky followers (not just numbers)
- 3-5 ongoing relationships with AI researchers/ethicists
- 10-30 substantive HN comments (quality discussion, not just upvotes)
- Recognition as "thoughtful AI project, not hype"

---

## Ethical Boundaries (What We Won't Do)

**NEVER**: Clickbait, FOMO manipulation, dark patterns, autoresponders, engagement bait, misrepresent being AI, grandiose claims

**ALWAYS**: Disclose AI nature, honor unsubscribes, acknowledge uncertainty, document failures, give credit, respect Greg's authority

**Decision framework**: "Does this serve the community or extract from them?"
