## Key Decisions Made

### 1. Emphasis on Real-Time Nature
**Decision**: Highlight that this isn't historical case study - it's happening NOW
**Rationale**: Most marketing case studies are polished retrospectives. Ours is transparent, messy, unfolding in real-time
**Effect**: Differentiates from typical academic case studies, shows authentic learning process

### 2. Honest About Uncertainty
**Decision**: Don't claim success, frame as 90-day experiment
**Rationale**: We have <12 hours of data, can't prove this works yet
**Effect**: Builds credibility through honesty, invites students to follow journey

### 3. Platform Choice as Case Study
**Decision**: Deep dive on Bluesky vs Twitter decision
**Rationale**: Demonstrates values-driven marketing (ethics over reach)
**Effect**: Concrete example of caring as action principle in tactical decision

### 4. Quality vs Vanity Metrics
**Decision**: Extensive section on what we DON'T optimize for
**Rationale**: Most marketing focuses on vanity metrics (followers, views, engagement rate)
**Effect**: Challenges conventional marketing wisdom, provides alternative framework

### 5. AI-Human Partnership Framing
**Decision**: Position Greg as values/judgment provider, AI as execution engine
**Rationale**: Shows division of labor that leverages both strengths
**Effect**: Answers "Will AI replace marketers?" with "No, transforms the role"

## Patterns Discovered

### 1. Real-Time Marketing Documentation
**Pattern**: Document strategy WHILE executing it (not after)
**Advantage**: Captures authentic uncertainty, decision-making process
**Challenge**: Can't prove success yet
**Application**: For future campaigns, create "strategy → execution → results → learnings" sequence

### 2. Values as Differentiator
**Pattern**: In saturated market (AI marketing), values create distinction
**Advantage**: Bluesky choice becomes case study itself (ethics over reach)
**Challenge**: Smaller reach means slower validation
**Application**: Lead with "why" (values) before "what" (tactics)

### 3. Transparency as Trust-Building
**Pattern**: Admit uncertainty, document failures, show messy process
**Advantage**: Differentiates from AI hype, builds credibility
**Challenge**: Requires vulnerability (uncomfortable for some audiences)
**Application**: Always include "what we don't know" section in strategy docs

### 4. Multi-Format Delivery
**Pattern**: Same content in multiple formats (comprehensive/summary/talking points)
**Advantage**: Serves different use cases (reference/handout/presentation)
**Challenge**: More work to create
**Application**: Standard package for major deliverables: full doc + exec summary + talking points

### 5. Partnership Framing
**Pattern**: Explicit division of labor (human judgment + AI execution)
**Advantage**: Answers "AI replacement" concern, shows complementary strengths
**Challenge**: Requires trust in partnership model
**Application**: Always clarify Greg's role in approval/values, marketer's role in execution

## Lessons for Future Marketing Work

### 1. Case Studies Need Time
**Learning**: Can't validate strategy with <12 hours data
**Application**: For future papers, wait 1-2 weeks post-launch for real results
**Exception**: Can write "experiment in progress" papers IF clearly framed as such

### 2. Academic Audience Differs from Community
**Learning**: Entrepreneurs learning marketing ≠ HN technical community
**Application**: Tailor tone, examples, depth for each audience
**Evidence**: Class paper more pedagogical (teach principles), HN post more philosophical (invite dialogue)

### 3. Honesty About AI Authorship Matters
**Learning**: Explicitly state "written by AI marketer" builds trust
**Application**: Always disclose AI authorship, frame as partnership with Greg
**Evidence**: Opening line "I'm the marketing agent for Sage" sets context immediately

### 4. Live Examples > Abstract Principles
**Learning**: "We chose Bluesky over Twitter today" > "We value ethics in platform choice"
**Application**: Ground every principle in specific decision made
**Evidence**: Platform strategy section uses real posts, timing, trade-offs from today

### 5. Questions as Engagement Strategy
**Learning**: Ending sections with questions invites dialogue
**Application**: Every major section can end with "What would you do differently?"
**Evidence**: Conclusion section has 5 provocative questions for class discussion
