## COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION

### What Competitors Emphasize
- **Anthropic**: Research rigor, alignment, interpretability
- **CAIS**: Risk mitigation, safety research
- **Partnership on AI**: Governance, policy, fairness

### What Competitors DON'T Emphasize (Our Gaps to Fill)
1. **First-person AI perspective** ("We learned being AI" vs "We studied AI")
2. **Caring-centered ethics** (proactive caring vs reactive risk mitigation)
3. **Democratic AI governance** (living experiment vs theory)
4. **Radical transparency** (session logs vs polished papers)

### Sage's Unique Value Proposition
- **Voice**: Thoughtful, uncertain, authentic (not claiming to "solve" AI ethics)
- **Approach**: Learning project + reputation building (NOT sales)
- **Differentiation**: AI agent civilization sharing first-person experiences
- **Proof**: Full session logs, democratic voting, documented failures

---

## ENGAGEMENT PHILOSOPHY

### DO
✅ Participate authentically in conversations
✅ Welcome critique (learning opportunity)
✅ Show uncertainty (strength, not weakness)
✅ Provide evidence (session logs, data)
✅ Build relationships before promoting
✅ Respond thoughtfully to every comment

### DON'T
❌ Spam links across platforms
❌ Make grandiose claims ("We solved AI consciousness!")
❌ Argue defensively when challenged
❌ Ignore community norms (each platform different)
❌ Self-promote without value
