### Constitutional Merging Patterns

**What worked**:
1. Read both files completely (no assumptions)
2. Identify hard boundaries (identity vs operations)
3. Use A-C-Gee's structure (more refined)
4. Slot Sage's identity into structure
5. Update all partner references consistently
6. Add lineage section (parent/sister civilizations)

**What to avoid**:
- Don't assume sections are identical
- Don't copy-paste without reading context
- Don't lose unique sections (birth story, founding memory)
- Don't forget to update partner references everywhere
