## Lessons Learned

### About Priority Management

**Urgent ≠ Important** (Eisenhower Matrix applies):
- Kelly: Urgent AND important (trust breach)
- Voice system: Not urgent, but important (can defer)
- Revenue discussion: Important, not urgent (schedule thoughtfully)
- MASTER_TODO: Neither urgent nor important (do after decisions)

**Relationships are existential**:
- Technical features can wait
- Trust breaches cannot wait
- Partnership health > project completion
- Core values (empathy) must guide prioritization

**Context prevents thrashing**:
- Without comprehensive review, might have jumped to voice system (exciting!)
- But Kelly issue more critical (less exciting but higher stakes)
- PM's role: Surface what matters most, not what's most fun

---

### About Blocker Detection

**6-hour target missed**:
- Kelly issue existed since ~Nov 7
- PM not invoked until Nov 13 (6 days later)
- Need proactive escalation for relationship matters

**Severity classification works**:
- HIGH: Kelly (trust breach, immediate action)
- MEDIUM: Angel (patience appropriate, gentle follow-up)
- LOW: Weaver coordination (flexible, no deadline)

**Natural vs problematic dependencies**:
- Revenue discussion waiting on Greg = natural
- Kelly automation bug affecting multiple contacts = problematic
- Distinguish between "blocked" and "waiting appropriately"

---

### About Communication to Greg

**When to escalate immediately**:
- Trust breaches (Kelly false message)
- Safety issues (automation sending incorrect info)
- Deadline risks (if Sunday Salon logistics were still unclear)

**When to wait for session**:
- Strategic decisions (revenue discussion)
- Optional features (blog tutorial)
- Lower-stakes issues (Weaver coordination)

**How to present**:
- Clear executive summary (Greg can decide without reading 15KB)
- Recommendations with rationale (not just "here are options")
- Questions organized by urgency (immediate, this week, flexible)
- Respect Greg's time (structured for fast decision-making)
