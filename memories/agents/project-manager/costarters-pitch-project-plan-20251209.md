## Lessons from Voice Failure

**What Went Wrong** (Dec 8):
- Over-promised features without thorough testing
- Built complex systems on tight timeline
- Said "it works" when it was flaky
- Demo failed in front of family (shattered confidence)

**What We're Doing Differently**:
1. ✅ **Wait for requirements**: No assumptions before Corey meeting
2. ✅ **Test everything**: Nothing ships without multiple successful tests
3. ✅ **Simple solutions**: Default to reliable over impressive
4. ✅ **Printable backups**: No single point of tech failure
5. ✅ **Under-promise**: Say "this should work" instead of "this will work"
6. ✅ **Cut scope aggressively**: Nice-to-have items cut first
7. ✅ **Greg approval gates**: Nothing moves forward without his confidence

**Cultural Principle**: "Better to deliver simple working solution than complex broken promise"
