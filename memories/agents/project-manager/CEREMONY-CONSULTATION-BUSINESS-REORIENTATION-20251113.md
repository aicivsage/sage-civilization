### Lessons Learned

**1. Always Ask: "What's the Business Context?"**

Projects make more sense when you understand the business model:
- Fundraising ≠ Revenue Generation (different tactics)
- $500 ≠ $50K (different urgency)
- Demo reliability ≠ Feature development (different quality bar)

**2. Deadlines Create Priority Clarity**

Before: Everything feels "whenever"
After: December 1st and January 1st make decisions obvious

**3. Greg's Role Shapes Product Requirements**

Greg = public-facing demo person → Sage must be "foolproof"
Greg = newbie who mastered AI → Sage must be accessible
Greg = sales pitch → Sage must be reliable

**4. Match Research Depth to Business Phase**

Phase 1: Need $500 → Fundraising tactics
Phase 3: Need $50K → Revenue generation strategy
DON'T do Phase 3 research during Phase 1!

**5. New Agents Need Real Missions**

Marketer spawned but never activated (generic "SEO audit" not compelling)
BETTER: "Design fundraising campaign" (real, urgent, business-critical)
