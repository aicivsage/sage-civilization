# 🎯 Pitch Structure Best Practices - CoStarters Research

**Date**: December 9, 2025
**Research Agent**: researcher
**Purpose**: Guide Greg's 5-minute CoStarters graduation pitch preparation

---

## Executive Summary

**Key Finding**: Community pitches (like CoStarters) require **story-driven problem-solution structure** that balances emotional connection with business credibility. Unlike investor pitches (ROI-focused), CoStarters audiences want to understand what you do, see your passion, and know how to help.

**Recommended Structure**: Modified "Pixar Pitch" framework
- **Time allocation**: 30s hook + 2min problem + 1.5min solution + 45s traction + 15s CTA
- **Visual aids**: 6-8 slides maximum (preferably 6)
- **Q&A focus**: Customer acquisition, differentiation, ROI proof

---

## CoStarters Pitch Party Format

**Audience**: Peers, local business owners, community supporters, potential customers, family/friends
**Tone**: Celebratory + supportive (NOT high-stakes investor pitch)
**Goals**: Share journey, gain feedback, find first customers, build network
**Format**: 5-7 minutes per presenter, informal Q&A

**Evaluation Criteria**:
- Clarity of business concept (can a 12-year-old understand?)
- Evidence of customer validation
- Realistic understanding of challenges
- Clear next steps
- Authenticity and passion

---

## Recommended Pitch Structure: Modified Pixar Pitch

**Why This Works**: Creates emotional connection while communicating business fundamentals

**Structure**:
1. **Once upon a time...** (Context: who you serve) - *30 seconds*
2. **Every day...** (Problem/pain point) - *1 minute*
3. **One day...** (Your insight/turning point) - *30 seconds*
4. **Because of that...** (Your solution) - *1.5 minutes*
5. **Until finally...** (Results/traction/proof) - *45 seconds*
6. **Ever since then...** (Call to action) - *15 seconds*

**Example for AI-CIV Workshops**:
> "Once upon a time, there were small business owners and educators who wanted to use AI but felt overwhelmed. Every day, they'd read about AI taking jobs or requiring expensive consultants. One day, I discovered that AI collaboration—not replacement—creates better outcomes. Because of that, I created workshops that teach people to work WITH AI as a thinking partner. Until finally, beta testers reported 300% productivity gains and newfound confidence. Ever since then, my mission has been to democratize AI collaboration—and I need your help connecting me with schools, nonprofits, and small businesses."

## Time Allocation Best Practices

**Rule of Thirds**:
- Opening Hook: 30 seconds (10%)
- Problem/Context: 2 minutes (40%)
- Solution: 1.5 minutes (30%)
- Traction/Proof: 45 seconds (15%)
- Call-to-Action: 15 seconds (5%)

**Practice target**: 4:30 (leaves 30-second buffer)

**Critical Guideline**: Spend MORE time on problem than solution (people buy solutions to problems they feel)

---

## Opening Hook Techniques

1. **Startling Statistic**: "85% of workers will need to reskill by 2030, but 70% of training budgets got cut"
2. **Personal Story**: "Last year, I watched my friend get fired because AI did her job better—then I taught her to use AI, and she got hired at 2x salary"
3. **Bold Claim**: "What if AI could triple your productivity in 3 hours for less than your monthly software subscription?"
4. **Provocative Question**: "Raise your hand if you're afraid AI will take your job. Keep it up if you know how to make AI work FOR you instead."

---

## Visual Aid Guidelines

**Optimal**: 6 slides (maximum 8)

**Recommended Structure**:
1. Title slide (identity)
2. Problem statement (build empathy)
3. Your insight (unique angle)
4. Solution (what you do)
5. Traction (proof/results)
6. Call-to-action (contact + ask)

**Design Principles**:
- **Rule of One**: One idea, one image, one data point per slide
- **80/20 ratio**: 80% visual, 20% text
- **Font size**: 30pt minimum body, 60pt+ headlines
- **High contrast**: Dark text on light background or vice versa

**Alternative**: One-page handout with MORE details (pricing, testimonials, case studies)

**Hybrid Approach (RECOMMENDED)**: 6 slides for presentation + detailed handout for after

## Q&A Preparation

**Most Common Questions**:
1. "How will you find customers?" → Specific channels: CoStarters network, LinkedIn, partnerships
2. "What makes you different?" → Human-guided, customized, ongoing support, ethical frameworks
3. "Isn't this too expensive?" → ROI calculation (if saves 5 hrs/week, pays for itself in 2 weeks)
4. "Can this scale?" → Phased: workshops → train-the-trainer → licensing → online courses
5. "How much revenue so far?" → Honest: "Pre-revenue, validated concept with beta users"

**AI-Specific Questions**:
1. "Isn't AI dangerous/unethical?" → "That's why I teach collaboration with ethical frameworks"
2. "Won't AI replace you?" → "AI can't replace human judgment—as AI improves, literacy becomes MORE critical"
3. "What if technology changes?" → "I teach principles, not specific tools—works across platforms"
4. "Why not free YouTube videos?" → "Workshops provide customization, troubleshooting, and accountability"

**How to Handle "I Don't Know"**:
- "Great question I hadn't considered—can I follow up after researching?"
- "I don't have data on that yet—focused on validating [X] first"
- "Honestly, I don't know yet. What I DO know is [pivot to strength]"

**Answer Length**: 30-45 seconds each
