## 1. Florida LLC Formation Requirements

### Filing Process

**Authority**: Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz.org)
**Primary Method**: Online e-filing at dos.fl.gov/sunbiz

### Required Documents

**Articles of Organization must include:**
- LLC name (must include "Limited Liability Company," "LLC," or "L.L.C.")
- Principal business address
- Registered agent name and Florida street address (not P.O. box)
- Registered agent signature accepting appointment
- Name and address of person forming the LLC
- Names and addresses of members/managers
- Member or authorized representative signature
- Contact email
- Optional: Desired effective date (within 90 days of filing)

| Item | Cost |

| Registered agent designation | $25 |
| **Total initial filing** | **$125** |
| Optional certified copy | $30 |

**Annual Requirements:**
- Annual Report: $138.75 (due May 1 each year)
- Late filing penalty: $400
- Failure to file: Administrative dissolution

### Timeline

- **Online filing**: Few days to 1 week typical
- **Mail filing**: 2-4 weeks
- **Processing order**: First-come, first-served

### Registered Agent Requirements

**Must have:**
- Physical Florida street address (P.O. boxes prohibited)

**Options:**

## 2. Florida Tax Advantages (MAJOR CONSIDERATION)

### No State Income Tax - Game Changer

**Florida has NO personal income tax.**

**Impact on Entity Choice:**

| Entity | Federal Tax | Florida State Tax | Total Tax Burden |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|------------------|
| **LLC (default)** | Pass-through to personal (0-37%) | **NONE** | Federal only |
| **LLC → S-Corp** | Pass-through to personal (0-37%) | **NONE** | Federal only |
| **LLC → C-Corp** | Corporate (21%) + Personal on dividends (0-20%) | Corporate (5.5%) + **NONE** on dividends | Federal + 5.5% corporate |

**Key Insight**: Florida's lack of personal income tax **reduces the double taxation penalty of C-Corps** compared to states with income tax.

**Example Calculation (C-Corp in Florida vs California):**

$100,000 corporate profit distributed as dividends:

**Florida C-Corp:**
- Corporate tax: $21,000 (federal) + $5,500 (FL corporate) = $26,500
- Dividend tax: $15,000 (federal qualified dividend rate) + $0 (no FL income tax) = $15,000
- Total tax: $41,500 (41.5% effective rate)

**California C-Corp (comparison):**
- Corporate tax: $21,000 (federal) + $8,840 (CA corporate 8.84%) = $29,840
- Dividend tax: $15,000 (federal) + $13,300 (CA income tax 13.3%) = $28,300
- Total tax: $58,140 (58.14% effective rate)

**Florida C-Corp saves 16.64 percentage points vs California!**

### Florida Corporate Income Tax

**Rate**: 5.5% on Florida-source net income

**Exemptions**:
- First $50,000 of taxable income is exempt from franchise tax component
- Only applies if LLC elects C-Corporation taxation

**Applies to**:
- C-Corporations (by default)
- LLCs that elect C-Corp taxation
- Does NOT apply to default LLCs or S-Corps (pass-through entities)

### Sales Tax

**Rate**: 6% state rate + 0-1.5% county discretionary surtax (varies by county)

**Application to Consulting/Robot Demonstration Business:**
- **Consulting services**: Generally NOT subject to sales tax in Florida
- **Product sales** (robots, kits): TAXABLE
- **Training/education services**: Generally exempt
- **Demonstration fees**: Likely exempt (service, not tangible goods)

**Requirement**: Obtain Annual Resale Certificate for Sales Tax if selling tangible goods

**Registration**: Through Florida Department of Revenue

### Self-Employment Tax

Applies equally across all states (federal tax):
- 15.3% on self-employment income (Social Security + Medicare)
- Applies to LLC members, sole proprietors
- S-Corp shareholders pay on salary only (not distributions)
- C-Corp shareholders do NOT pay on dividends

**Florida provides no relief from SE tax, but:**
- No additional state SE tax (unlike some states)
- No disability insurance tax (unlike CA, NY, etc.)

## 3. Florida State Benefits and Income Limits

**CRITICAL NOTE**: Access issues with Florida DCF and SSA websites limited research. The information below is partial and requires professional verification.

### Federal SSDI Baseline

**Federal SSDI (Social Security Disability Insurance):**
- Uses monthly Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) threshold
- 2024 SGA: ~$1,550/month for non-blind disabled individuals
- 2025 SGA: Likely ~$1,620/month (adjusts annually)
- **NO $3,000 annual limit exists in federal SSDI rules**

**Trial Work Period (TWP):**
- First 9 months earning >TWP threshold (~$1,050/month) in rolling 60-month period
- During TWP: Keep full benefits regardless of earnings
- After TWP: Benefits continue if earnings <SGA

**Self-Employment Income:**
- Counted differently than wages
- Net earnings from self-employment (after expenses) used
- May deduct Impairment-Related Work Expenses (IRWE)

### Florida Medicaid (MEDS-AD)

**Program**: Medicaid for Aged and Disabled (Regular Medicaid)

**Eligibility Limits** (as of 4/1/25-3/31/26):
- **Income limit**: $1,149/month for single individuals
- **Asset limit**: $5,000 for individuals
- **Functional requirement**: Needs help with Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)

**SSDI Interaction**:
- SSI recipients automatically eligible for Regular Medicaid
- SSDI benefits counted as income toward $1,149/month limit
- Working disabled individuals may qualify for Medicaid Buy-In programs

**If Greg receives $3K/year income limit notice, this is likely:**
1. **NOT federal SSDI** (uses monthly limits ~$18,000+/year)
2. **Possibly Florida Medicaid** (annual equivalent would be ~$13,788/year for MEDS-AD)
3. **Possibly housing assistance program** (see below)
4. **Possibly misunderstanding of benefit interactions**

### Florida SNAP (Food Assistance)

**Household Size Impact**: SNAP eligibility depends on total household size

**For household with disabled member:**
- **Net income test only** (no gross income test)
- Net income limit (Oct 2025-Sep 2026): $2,680/month for household of 4
- Higher resource limit: $4,500 in countable assets (vs $2,750 standard)
- Enhanced shelter deduction: All shelter costs >50% of income (vs $744 cap)

**Business Income:**
- Self-employment income counted as net earnings (after expenses)

### Florida Housing Assistance

**Programs likely include:**
- Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher (federal, administered by local housing authorities)
- State Housing Initiatives Partnership (SHIP)
- HUD 811 Project Rental Assistance (for disabled individuals)

**Income Limits typically:**
- Tied to Area Median Income (AMI) for county
- Range from 30% AMI (extremely low) to 80% AMI (low income)
- Tampa-St. Petersburg MSA typical limits:
  - 30% AMI (1 person): ~$17,850/year
  - 50% AMI (1 person): ~$29,750/year
  - 80% AMI (1 person): ~$47,600/year

**CRITICAL**: The "$3,000/year" limit could be a local housing authority specific rule for maintaining eligibility while working. This would be MUCH more restrictive than typical programs.

## 4. Florida LLC Operating Agreement Requirements

### Legal Requirement

**Florida Statutes Chapter 605 governs LLCs**.

**Operating Agreement:**
- **NOT required by Florida law** to file with state
- **STRONGLY RECOMMENDED** for all LLCs (especially multi-member)
- Private document between members (not public record)
- Controls internal operations

### Recommended Provisions

**Based on standard Florida LLC practice:**

1. **Management Structure:**
   - **Member-managed**: All members participate in decisions (default)
   - **Manager-managed**: Designated manager(s) handle operations
   - For Greg+Corey: Likely member-managed initially

2. **Capital Contributions:**
   - Initial contributions (cash, property, services)
   - Future contribution requirements
   - Treatment of loans vs capital

3. **Profit and Loss Allocation:**
   - Distribution schedule and priorities
   - Tax allocation (may differ from cash distributions)
   - Preferred returns (if any)

4. **Transfer Restrictions:**
   - Right of first refusal for other members
   - Prohibited transfers (to competitors, etc.)
   - Buyout provisions on death/disability

5. **Dissolution and Exit:**
   - Triggering events for dissolution
   - Buyout formulas
   - Wind-down procedures

6. **SSDI Protection Provisions (CRITICAL FOR GREG):**

   **Recommended language to protect benefit eligibility:**

   ```
   Special Provisions for Member [Greg]:

   a) Income Limitation: Notwithstanding any other provision,
      distributions to [Greg] in any calendar year shall not
      exceed $[amount] without [Greg's] prior written consent.

   b) Benefit Protection: The Members acknowledge that [Greg]
      receives government disability benefits subject to income
      limits. All Members agree to structure distributions and
      compensation to [Greg] in a manner that preserves benefit
      eligibility to the extent possible.

   c) Override Right: [Greg] shall have the unilateral right to
      decline any distribution, defer compensation, or request
      alternative structuring to maintain benefit eligibility.

   d) Corey's Priority: In any year where [Greg] cannot accept
      distributions due to benefit limits, [Corey] shall receive
      100% of distributable cash flow, with [Greg's] share
      credited as deferred compensation or retained in the company.
   ```

   **Why this matters:**
   - Prevents accidental benefit loss from automatic distributions
   - Allows Greg to control timing and amount of income
   - Enables Corey to receive returns even if Greg cannot
   - Creates legal framework for benefit-compliant operation

### Manager-Managed vs Member-Managed

**Member-Managed (recommended for 2-person partnership):**
- All members have equal management authority
- All can bind the LLC
- All participate in decisions
- Simpler structure
- Standard for small partnerships

**Manager-Managed (consider if Greg needs to limit active participation):**
- Designated manager(s) run day-to-day operations
- Non-manager members are passive investors
- May help characterize Greg's role as passive (for benefit purposes)
- **CAUTION**: SSA may still count LLC income as self-employment

**Recommendation for Greg+Corey:**
- Likely **member-managed** with special income limitation provisions
- Consider consulting benefits attorney before choosing structure
- Manager-managed may NOT protect SSDI (SSA looks through to ownership)

### Asset Protection in Florida

**Florida offers strong asset protection:**

1. **Charging Order Protection:**
   - Creditor of LLC member limited to "charging order" against distributions
   - Creditor cannot force sale of membership or take control
   - Florida provides strong single-member LLC protection (unlike some states)

2. **Florida Homestead Exemption:**
   - Unlimited homestead exemption (separate from LLC)
   - May protect personal residence from business liabilities

3. **Liability Shield:**
   - Members not personally liable for LLC debts (unless personally guaranteed)
   - Protects personal assets from business liabilities

**Important**: Asset protection does NOT shield from:
- Personal guarantees on LLC debts
- Personal torts (negligence outside LLC scope)
- Tax liabilities
- Fraudulent transfers
