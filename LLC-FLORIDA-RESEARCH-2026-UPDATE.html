5. Operating Agreement Checklist (Sunbiz Compliance)

What Florida Sunbiz DOES NOT Require

Good news: Operating agreements are NOT filed with Florida Division of Corporations.

What IS public record:

  • Articles of Organization (LLC name, registered agent, principal address)
  • Annual Reports (member/manager names and addresses)
  • Registered agent changes

What is PRIVATE:

  • Operating agreement (internal document)
  • Financial arrangements between members
  • Profit/loss allocation formulas
  • Greg's SSDI status and income limitations

Operating Agreement Checklist

REQUIRED (Legal Enforceability):

  • LLC name and formation date
  • Member names and addresses
  • Ownership percentages (99% Corey / 1% Greg)
  • Management structure designation
  • Capital contributions (initial amounts from each member)
  • Signature of all members
  • Execution date

STRONGLY RECOMMENDED (SSDI Protection):

  • Profit and loss allocation method
  • Distribution policy (timing, approval process)
  • Income limitation provisions for Greg (annual cap, decline right, reallocation to Corey)
  • Benefit protection acknowledgment
  • Business expense definition (what expenses are deducted before member allocations)
  • Voting rights and major decision thresholds
  • Amendment procedures
  • Dissolution triggers and procedures
  • Buyout formula and payment terms
  • Anti-override provision (Greg's SSDI protections require his consent to amend)

Accountable Plan for Expense Reimbursement

IRS Accountable Plan rules (important for Greg's SSDI):

If LLC reimburses Greg for business expenses under IRS "accountable plan":

  • ✅ Reimbursements are NOT taxable income to Greg
  • ✅ Do NOT appear on Greg's K-1
  • ✅ Do NOT count toward SSDI income limits

Example:

Greg buys laptop for workshop facilitation: $1,200
Greg submits receipt to LLC with business purpose documentation
LLC reimburses Greg $1,200 from business funds

This $1,200 is NOT Greg's income (it's reimbursement of his expense)
This $1,200 does NOT count toward $3K limit

Why this matters for SSDI:

  • Allows LLC to cover Greg's workshop-related costs (laptop, phone, travel, branded clothing)
  • These reimbursements do NOT count as Greg's income
  • Reduces Greg's out-of-pocket costs without affecting benefit eligibility
  • Properly structured, could provide Greg significant non-cash benefit
