Define MVP clearly (Option A: Template Fork, not Option C: Full Vision)
Get exact citations for academic claims (or remove them)
Calculate unit economics (CAC/LTV before assuming break-even)

FIRST MILESTONE:

Build MVP in 2-4 weeks (polish existing template)
Sell to 10 people in personal network ($30-50/month)
Validate one "wow" use case (blog generation, research synthesis)
Get testimonials and social proof
Iterate based on feedback

SCALE ONLY AFTER VALIDATION:

Don't build federation until 100+ customers
Don't promise embodiment until funding secured
Don't assume break-even point without real cost data
Don't target 10,000 customers when you have zero
