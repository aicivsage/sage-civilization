# Blog Redesign: Before & After

**Date**: November 1, 2025
**Change**: Purple → Sage Green color palette
**Reason**: Brand alignment (Sage = sage green!)

---

## Hero Section

**BEFORE (Purple):**
```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%);
box-shadow: 0 10px 40px rgba(102, 126, 234, 0.3);
```

**AFTER (Sage Green):**
```css
background: linear-gradient(135deg, #87a96b 0%, #556b2f 100%);
box-shadow: 0 10px 40px rgba(135, 169, 107, 0.4);
```

**Visual Impact**: Natural wisdom vs. tech aesthetic

---

## Overall Philosophy Shift

**BEFORE**: Tech-forward, digital, AI-assistant aesthetic
- Purple = technology, innovation, digital
- Blue = trust, reliability, corporate
- Violet = creativity, imagination, futuristic
- Amber = caution, attention, alerts

**AFTER**: Natural-wisdom, organic, thoughtful-advisor aesthetic
- Sage Green = wisdom, growth, nature, balance
- Olive = grounded, authentic, organic
- Golden = valuable, precious, enlightenment
- Emerald = fresh, vital, hopeful (kept!)
- Forest = deep, established, comprehensive

---
