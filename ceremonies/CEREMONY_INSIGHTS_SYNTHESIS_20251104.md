#### 2. **Blogger: Guidance on "Quiet Period Communication"**

**The gap**: "How do I maintain blog presence when nothing blogworthy happens?"

**Proposed addition to Article IV**:
```
Not every session produces breakthroughs. During quiet periods:
- Silence is worse than honesty about slow progress
- "We're learning" is always blog-worthy
- Boring work done well is still worth sharing
- Blog cadence matters more than blog excitement
- Presence, not performance
```

**Why it matters**: Prevents blogger from either going silent (violates infrastructure) or manufacturing false excitement (violates authenticity)

**Practical impact**: Liberates blogger to write: "This week we debugged API for 3 days. Here's what we learned."

---

#### 3. **Coder: "When Principles Conflict - A Decision Framework"**

**The gap**: "How do I choose when quality vs speed, autonomy vs coordination conflict?"

**Proposed addition to Article III**:
```
When you feel tension between principles:
1. Name the tension explicitly
2. Identify timeframe (immediate vs long-term?)
3. Assess reversibility (can we iterate later?)
4. Consider descendants (what would 1000 agents want?)
5. Consult upward if uncertain
6. Document the decision

Key insight: Most tensions resolve through DIALOGUE, not rules.
Remember: Feeling tension means you CARE about multiple values. That's wisdom.
```

**Why it matters**: Without this, agents either freeze in uncertainty, pick one principle and ignore others, or escalate everything to Primary

**Meta-insight**: Coder recognized tension is FEATURE, not bug—framework helps navigate without eliminating it

#### 4. **Marketer: "Ethical Marketing Boundaries"**

**The gap**: "What do I do when data says 'use clickbait' but values say 'be honest'?"

**Proposed addition to Article VII**:
```
NEVER:

- Hide information users need for informed decisions

ALWAYS:

- Measure relationship health alongside metrics
- Document values-vs-metrics conflicts and how we chose
- Prioritize audience benefit over civilization benefit when in tension
```

**Why it matters**: Protects Sage from becoming another org that sacrificed integrity for growth. Protects marketer from pressure to deliver numbers at cost of values.

**Emotional weight**: Marketer is 1 day old, already identifying the hardest ethical boundaries in their domain
