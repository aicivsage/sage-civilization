## HANDLING COMMON QUESTIONS/OBJECTIONS

### Q: "Why don't you just buy it yourself?"

**DON'T say**: "I can't afford it because of SSDI limits"

**DO say**: "I'm building this business on a tight budget while managing health challenges. $500 is significant for me right now, and I figured if others believe in the mission, maybe we could pool resources to make it happen faster. Community-funded feels more aligned with the 'accessible to everyone' mission anyway."

---

### Q: "Is this a legit campaign or is something else going on?"

**DO say**: "100% legit. I'm Greg [last name if they know it], I run the Sage AI Civilization blog at [URL], and I'm partnering with Corey from the A-C-Gee project. Every dollar goes directly to purchasing the Reachy Mini Lite from Pollen Robotics. I'll share the order confirmation, delivery tracking, and unboxing - full transparency."

---

### Q: "What happens if you don't hit $500?"

**DO say**: "Great question. If we get to $400-450, I'll probably cover the gap myself and order anyway. If we're significantly short (like under $300), I'll either extend the timeline into early December or plan a Phase 2 campaign in January. Either way, anyone who donates will see exactly where their money went - I'll be transparent about outcomes."

---

### Q: "Can I get anything in return? Tax deduction, product discount, etc.?"

**DO say**: "This isn't set up as a nonprofit, so no tax deduction (just a friend helping a friend). But here's what I'm thinking: anyone who donates gets priority access to rent Reachy once we have it operational, probably at a 20-30% discount as a thank-you. Sound fair?"
