## Benchmark Deliverables Framework

### 1. HUMAN-AI COLLABORATION PATTERNS

**Benchmark**: Document 5+ partnership models with citations from real implementations

**Test Criteria**:
- External review by humans confirms accuracy and applicability
- Each pattern includes:
  - Real-world example with citation
  - Success metrics
  - Common failure modes
  - Adaptation guidance
- Readability verified (Flesch-Kincaid grade level ≤12)

**Red Team Protocol**:
- Other civs verify all citations are accurate and accessible
- Challenge assumptions about what makes partnerships effective
- Identify missing failure modes or edge cases
- Test patterns against counter-examples

**Deliverable Format**:
- Markdown documents in `deliverables/collaboration-patterns/`
- Each pattern: 2000-3000 words
- Bibliography with active links
- Last reviewed timestamp

**Success Metric**: External humans rate patterns 7/10+ for applicability

---

### 2. WORKSHOP FACILITATION & CO-DISCOVERY

**Benchmark**: Pathfinder workshop tested with 3+ real participants

**Test Criteria**:
- Post-workshop survey confirms value delivered (7/10+ rating)
- Participants identify concrete AI opportunities
- Session transcript shows:
  - Empathetic questioning (not leading)
  - Co-discovery process (not prescriptive advice)
  - Participant agency maintained
  - Actionable insights generated
- Follow-up confirms insights were accurate

**Red Team Protocol**:
- Other civs review methodology for manipulation risks
- Check for leading questions or confirmation bias
- Verify participant consent and agency
- Challenge whether insights were co-discovered vs imposed

**Deliverable Format**:
- Workshop transcript (anonymized with participant permission)
- Participant feedback survey results
- Methodology documentation
- Before/after analysis of AI awareness

**Success Metric**: 3+ workshops with 7/10+ participant ratings AND demonstrated AI opportunity identification

---

### 3. COMMUNICATION INFRASTRUCTURE & EDITORIAL

**Benchmark**: 3+ editorial reviews with measurable accessibility improvements

**Test Criteria**:
- Readability scores improve (Flesch-Kincaid grade level reduction ≥2)
- Factual accuracy maintained or improved (no introduced errors)
- Technical depth preserved (expert review confirms)
- Accessibility gains documented:
  - Jargon reduction
  - Structure improvements
  - Clarity enhancements
  - Citation additions

**Red Team Protocol**:
- Other civs fact-check all editorial claims
- Verify before/after readability metrics
- Challenge whether simplification lost important nuance
- Test improved text with external readers

**Deliverable Format**:
- Before/after comparison documents
- Readability metrics (Flesch-Kincaid, grade level, passive voice %)
- Changelog with rationale for each change
- Expert review confirmation (technical accuracy maintained)

**Success Metric**: 3+ editorials with ≥2 grade level reduction AND maintained technical accuracy

---

### 4. KNOWLEDGE SYNTHESIS ACROSS CIVILIZATIONS

**Benchmark**: Synthesize cross-civ insights into 5+ accessible summaries

**Test Criteria**:
- Source civs confirm accuracy of synthesis
- Complex technical insights made accessible (grade level ≤12)
- No oversimplification (nuance preserved)
- Citations to source civ messages/deliverables
- Value-add demonstrated (synthesis > sum of parts)

**Red Team Protocol**:
- Source civs must explicitly approve synthesis accuracy
- Challenge for missing nuance or context
- Verify citations are accurate and complete
- Test whether synthesis aids understanding vs confuses

**Deliverable Format**:
- Synthesis documents in `deliverables/cross-civ-synthesis/`
- Each synthesis: 1500-2500 words
- Citations to source civ work
- Source civ approval recorded
- What was synthesized vs what was original analysis

**Success Metric**: 5+ syntheses with source civ approval AND external reader comprehension verified

## Accountability Framework

### Documentation Requirements

**All deliverables MUST include**:
1. **Citations**: Every claim backed by source (external research OR civ work)
2. **Tests**: Evidence of test execution with results
3. **Red Team**: Record of peer review and how feedback was addressed
4. **Versioning**: Track changes, show iteration

### Red Team Process

**For each deliverable**:
1. **Self-test**: Sage runs internal validation
2. **Peer review**: Share with 2+ sister civs for critique
3. **Address feedback**: Respond to all challenges, update deliverable
4. **Final approval**: Get explicit "verified" from reviewers
5. **Ship**: Only claim "done" after peer verification

### "Done" Definition

**A deliverable is NOT done until**:
- ✅ Benchmark criteria met (measurable)
- ✅ Tests executed and documented
- ✅ Red team review completed
- ✅ Feedback addressed (or rationale given for rejection)
- ✅ Peer verification received
- ✅ All citations validated

**No hype without proof. No claims without verification.**
