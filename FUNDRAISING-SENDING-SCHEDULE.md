## STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

### The Psychology of Sequencing

**Why not send to everyone at once?**

1. **Social proof builds momentum**: Early donations from Tier 1 → "5 people already donated!" → increases Tier 2 conversion
2. **Calibration opportunity**: Test messaging with closest contacts → adjust before broader outreach
3. **Energy management**: Responding to questions/thank-yous requires bandwidth (spread it out)
4. **Urgency creation**: Staggered sends → "4 days left" becomes more powerful for later tiers

**The flow**:
- Days 1-2: Tier 1 (close friends) → high conversion, early wins
- Days 3-4: Tier 2 (professional contacts) → show Tier 1 momentum
- Day 5: Public launch (blog post) → Tier 3 sees 60-70% progress
- Days 6-8: Follow-ups + final push → close the gap

## OPTIMAL TIMING RECOMMENDATIONS

### Best Times to Send Emails

**Weekday mornings** (8-10 AM): Highest open rates (people checking inbox at work start)
**Weekday afternoons** (2-4 PM): Good for follow-ups (after lunch, before end of day)
**Weekend mornings** (10 AM-12 PM): Casual timing (people checking personal email)

**Avoid**:
- Very early (<7 AM) or very late (>9 PM) - can feel intrusive
- Friday evenings (people disengaging for weekend)
- Monday very early (inbox overwhelm)

### Best Times to Post Social Media

**Bluesky/Twitter**:
- Morning: 8-9 AM (commute/coffee scrolling)
- Lunch: 12-1 PM (break time engagement)
- Evening: 6-8 PM (post-work relaxation)

**LinkedIn**:
- Tuesday-Thursday mornings (7-9 AM) - professional context
- Lunch hour (12-1 PM) - quick professional check-ins
- Avoid weekends (lower engagement)
