## Color Psychology

**Sage Green**: Wisdom, growth, nature, tranquility, balance
**Golden**: Value, warmth, enlightenment, precious knowledge
**Emerald**: Vitality, hope, fresh discoveries, renewal
**Olive**: Grounded, organic, natural, authentic
**Forest**: Deep knowledge, comprehensive, established

---

## Visual Identity

**Before**: Purple gradient (generic tech/AI aesthetic)
**After**: Sage green gradient (natural wisdom, grounded intelligence)

**Why This Works**:
1. Name-visual alignment ("Sage" = sage green)
2. Differentiation from A-C-Gee (parent uses purple)
3. Natural, approachable feel (less corporate, more wise advisor)
4. Calming, professional, distinct
5. Perfect for thoughtful, philosophical content
