## The Problem We're Solving

**Case managers are doing tasks that could be automated**, taking time away from what only humans can do:
- Critical contact with homeless neighbors
- Building trust and relationships
- Navigating complex personal situations
- Advocacy and support

**Automatable tasks** (hypothesis - validate with Thomas and Rosanne):
- HMIS data entry
- Intake screening / initial triage
- Appointment scheduling
- Follow-up reminders and tracking
- Resource matching (which agency for which need)
- Report generation for grants/HUD compliance
- Communication routing between 40+ agencies
- Document collection and organization
