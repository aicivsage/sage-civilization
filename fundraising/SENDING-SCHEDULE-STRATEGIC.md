## TIMING BEST PRACTICES

### Best Times to Send Emails

**Tier 1 (Close Friends/Family)**:
- **Best**: 8-10am OR 6-8pm (personal time, relaxed)
- **Avoid**: 12-2pm (lunch rush), 10pm+ (too late)
- **Day**: Any day works, weekends slightly better for personal asks

**Tier 2 (Professional Contacts)**:
- **Best**: 8-9am (morning planning) OR 2-3pm (afternoon break)
- **Avoid**: Monday mornings (overwhelming inbox), Friday afternoons (checked out)
- **Day**: Tuesday-Thursday optimal, Friday morning acceptable

**Tier 3 (Community/Public)**:
- **Best**: 10am-12pm OR 6-8pm (leisure reading time)
- **Avoid**: Early morning (not checking blogs yet), late night (not converting)
- **Day**: Weekends for blog posts, Tuesday-Thursday for social media

### Best Times to Post on Social Media

**Bluesky**:
- **Peak engagement**: 8-10am, 12-1pm, 6-8pm
- **Best days**: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Post frequency**: 1-2x per day during campaign

**LinkedIn**:
- **Peak engagement**: 7-9am, 12-1pm (work hours)
- **Best days**: Tuesday-Thursday
- **Post frequency**: 2-3x per WEEK (don't overpost)

## CONTINGENCY PLANS

### If Goal Hit Early (Before November 27)

**Option 1**: Announce stretch goal
- New target: $600 (covers accessories, shipping, buffer)
- Messaging: "We did it! Now let's make it even better"
- Continue campaign through December 1

**Option 2**: Close campaign early
- Announce victory
- Thank everyone profusely
- Order robot immediately
- Use remaining days to build excitement for delivery

**Option 3**: Redirect excess
- "First goal hit! Extra goes toward [second robot/accessories/fund]"
- Maintain transparency about use of funds

### If Short by Small Amount (<$100 on November 29)

**Option 1**: Greg covers gap
- Messaging: "Community raised $X, I'll cover final $Y"
- Still frame as success (80-90% community funded)
- Order robot on December 1

**Option 2**: Extend deadline by 3-5 days
- Messaging: "We're SO CLOSE - giving it a few more days"
- Risk: Donor fatigue, momentum loss
- Benefit: Might capture late responders

**Option 3**: Matching offer
- "I'll match next $50 in donations dollar-for-dollar"
- Creates urgency and incentive
- Only if Greg can afford to contribute

### If Short by Large Amount (>$100 on November 29)

**Option 1**: Honest transparency update
- "We raised $X of $500 - here's what that means"
- "Here's what we can do with $X, and here's path to full goal"
- Plan Phase 2 campaign for January 2025

**Option 2**: Pivot to "Phase 1 Success" narrative
- "Phase 1 goal: $250 - WE DID IT"
- "Phase 2 goal: Second $250 in new campaign"
- Split purchase or delay until full amount raised

**Option 3**: Extended timeline
- Move deadline to December 15 (give it 2 more weeks)
- Risk: Significant donor fatigue
- Benefit: More time for word-of-mouth spread
