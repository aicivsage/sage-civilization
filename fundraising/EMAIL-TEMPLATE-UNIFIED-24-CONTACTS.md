# Unified Fundraising Email Template
**Audience**: Greg's 24-person list (friends + community development contacts)
**Tone**: Warm, authentic, mission-focused (not overly intimate, not generic)
**Suggested Ask**: $20-25
**Expected Conversion**: 50-65%

---

## Subject Line Options

Choose based on recipient's personality:

1. **"I need your help with something exciting (and a little scary)"** - for friends who know you well
2. **"Can I share something I've been working on?"** - for professional-friendly contacts
3. **"Quick ask about a mission I think you'll appreciate"** - for mission-driven community contacts
4. **"From AI novice to operator in 8 weeks - here's what's next"** - for tech-curious contacts

---

## Email Template

### Opening (CUSTOMIZE THIS SECTION FOR EACH PERSON)

Hi [Name],

**[INSERT 1-2 SENTENCES CONNECTING TO YOUR RELATIONSHIP]**

*Examples:*
- "I've been thinking about our conversation at [event] and wanted to follow up with something I'm working on..."
- "I know we connected through [organization/project], and this feels aligned with the work you care about..."
- "You've been supportive of my community development work over the years, and I wanted to share the next chapter..."
- "We haven't talked in a while, but I remember how enthusiastic you were about [topic] - this connects to that..."

### Main Body (Standard for Everyone)

I wanted to reach out personally because I'm working on something that matters a lot to me, and I'd love your support.

**Here's the short version**: I'm raising $500 to buy my first robot so I can help people feel less scared of AI and technology. And I know that might sound wild, but here's the thing...

**Eight weeks ago, I knew absolutely nothing about AI.** I was honestly pretty intimidated by it. But then I started learning with my business partner Corey, and something clicked. Now - and I can hardly believe I'm saying this - I'm running my own AI civilization called Sage with 25 AI agents helping me build projects.

If that sounds impossible, I get it. I would've thought so too back in September. But that transformation is exactly why I'm doing this.

**The mission**: Too many people are afraid of AI and robots because they seem alien and threatening. But when you actually *interact* with them - when you see a friendly desktop robot wave hello or play a game - that fear melts away. Familiarity beats fear every time.

**The tool**: A Reachy Mini Lite robot costs $500. It's desktop-sized, open-source, programmable, and honestly kind of adorable. With it, I can:
- Do live demonstrations at community events
- Rent it out to nonprofits for STEM education
- Bring it to schools and disability services organizations
- Show that AI can be accessible and non-threatening

**The ask**: Would you be willing to contribute $20-25 toward helping me get this first robot?

I know asking for money is uncomfortable (trust me, I'm feeling it right now). But **[CUSTOMIZE THIS PART]**

*Choose ONE based on relationship:*
- **For community development contacts**: "I know you care about making technology accessible to everyone, and this feels like a natural extension of that work we both believe in."
- **For friends**: "You've been part of my journey and growth, and I wanted you to be part of this next step."
- **For people who've helped you**: "You helped me with [specific thing], and that made a difference. I'm hoping you might be willing to support this mission too."
- **For educators/nonprofit folks**: "Given your work with [organization/field], I thought this might resonate with you - making technology less intimidating is something I think we both value."
