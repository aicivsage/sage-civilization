### PHASE 2: Tailored Demo (30-40 min)

**Based on Thomas's answers, choose 2-3 examples to show:**

#### Option A: Grant Writing Demo ☐
**Show when Thomas mentions:**
- Grant deadlines
- Executive Director stretched thin
- Need more funding sources

**How to Show It:**
1. Hand him printed grant proposal example
2. "I gave Sage a grant RFP and our mission statement. It drafted this in 10 minutes."
3. Walk through: Executive summary, budget, outcomes
4. Emphasize: "This is a FIRST DRAFT. You'd still review, personalize, add stories. But instead of 6 hours from scratch, it's 1 hour of editing."
5. Time-saving: **6 hours → 1 hour = 5 hours saved**

---

#### Option B: Data Analysis Demo ☐
**Show when Thomas mentions:**
- Board reporting
- Funder reports
- Need to understand trends
- Point-in-Time Count or HMIS data

**How to Show It:**
1. Hand him printed data analysis example
2. "I fed Sage Point-in-Time Count data from 3 years. It found patterns, created recommendations."
3. Walk through: Trends, geographic patterns, actionable recommendations
4. Emphasize: "This isn't just charts - it's INSIGHTS. What do the numbers mean? What should you do about it?"
5. Time-saving: **4 hours → 15 minutes = 3.5 hours saved**

---

#### Option C: Board Report Demo ☐
**Show when Thomas mentions:**
- Monthly board meetings
- Executive Director spending hours compiling reports
- Need to show Board what's happening

**How to Show It:**
1. Hand him printed board report example
2. "I gave Sage data from shelter operations, financials, programs. It compiled this executive summary."
3. Walk through: Performance metrics, financials, strategic initiatives, impact stories
4. Emphasize: "Board gets the same quality report, but ED spends 30 minutes reviewing instead of 3 hours compiling."
5. Time-saving: **3 hours → 30 minutes = 2.5 hours saved**

---

#### Option D: Live Demo (if he's interested) ☐
**Only if Thomas wants to see it in action:**
1. Open terminal, start Claude
2. Show simple interaction: "Sage, draft a thank-you letter to a donor who gave $500 to our homeless coalition"
3. Let it generate in real-time (takes 30 seconds)
4. Show result, explain: "This is how easy it is - plain English, instant draft"
5. **WARNING:** Don't over-complicate. Keep it simple. One example is enough.

### PHASE 4: Next Steps (5-10 min)

**Frame It (No Pressure):**
- "This is exploratory - no pressure to decide today."
- "What would you need to see to know if this is worth pursuing?"
- "Who else at the Coalition should be part of this conversation?"

**Possible Outcomes:**

✅ **High Interest:**
- Schedule follow-up meeting
- Get data/documents for proof of concept
- Timeline: "Let's reconnect in 2 weeks after you've thought about it"

⚠️ **Moderate Interest:**
- "I'll discuss with the Board"
- "Let me think about it"
- Response: "No problem. What questions can I answer in the meantime?"

❌ **Low Interest:**
- "Not right now" or "Maybe later"
- Response: "I understand. Can I follow up in 3-6 months to see if timing is better?"

**Always End With:**
- "Thanks for your time, Thomas. Even if this isn't the right fit, I appreciate you exploring it with me."
- "Here's my contact info - feel free to reach out with questions anytime."
