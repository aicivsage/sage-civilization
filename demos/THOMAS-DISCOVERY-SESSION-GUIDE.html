Goal: Understand Coalition pain points, NOT pitch/demo

Your Opening Frame

"Thomas, I've been working with this AI system called Sage for a couple months now, and I keep thinking it might be useful for the Coalition. But before I get ahead of myself, I wanted to ask you about the day-to-day reality of running things.

I know you have a small staff doing multiple jobs. I'm curious - what are the biggest time-wasters? Where do you wish your team could spend LESS time on admin and MORE time on direct client care?

I'm not here to pitch you anything today. I just want to understand if there's a real problem AI could actually help solve."

How to Close the Meeting

"This is super helpful, Thomas. Let me take this back and think about whether AI could actually address these specific pain points. If it makes sense, I'll put together a quick demo focused on YOUR actual needs - not generic 'here's what AI can do' stuff.

Would it be okay if I follow up in a week or two with something concrete? And if it doesn't look like a good fit, I'll tell you that honestly too."

After the Meeting - Bring Back to Sage

Within 24 hours, create a session with Sage to:

  1. Share your notes - All the pain points, time estimates, staff examples
  2. Ask Sage to assess - Can AI actually solve these specific problems?
  3. If YES → Create targeted demo focused on Thomas's top 1-2 pain points
  4. If NO/PARTIAL → Be honest with Thomas about limitations

What Sage will need from you:

  • The 3 pain points (specific as possible)
  • Time estimates (hours per week/month)
  • Who does the work currently
  • What "good" looks like (desired outcome)

Mindset Reminders

✅ DO

  • Discover, not sell
  • Thomas is the expert
  • Silence is okay - let him think
  • Take notes openly (shows care)
  • "I don't know yet" is great
  • Honesty builds trust

❌ DON'T

  • Pitch/demo today
  • Promise what AI can't deliver
  • Minimize his concerns
  • Rush to solutions
  • Over-promise capabilities
  • Make it transactional

Success Metrics for This Meeting

This meeting is successful if:

  • You understand the top 3 pain points clearly
  • You have rough time estimates for each pain point
  • You know who's affected and how
  • Thomas feels HEARD (not sold to)
  • You have permission to follow up with targeted demo
  • You preserved the 10-year relationship (partnership, not transaction)

This meeting is NOT about:

  • Closing a sale
  • Proving AI's capabilities
  • Impressing Thomas with technical knowledge
  • Getting a commitment
