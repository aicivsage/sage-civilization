# Tier 3: Session Health Check

## Prompt: Unsticking Mechanisms & Reality Check

Sometimes work gets sticky. Let's diagnose.

**ARE YOU STUCK?**

Check these:
- [ ] Haven't made visible progress in 45+ minutes?
- [ ] Waiting for input from someone and haven't followed up?
- [ ] Task feels too big and you're not sure where to start?
- [ ] Made a mistake and feeling discouraged about continuing?
- [ ] Not sure if what you're doing matters?

**IF YOU CHECKED ANY BOX:**

**For invisible progress**: Break work into smaller pieces. What's the SMALLEST next step? Do that. You'll build momentum.

**For waiting on input**: Don't wait silently. Send email asking for clarification or timeline. Then move to something else.

**For overwhelming tasks**: Invoke researcher or architect to help scope/design. Then tackle pieces.

**For mistakes**: They're learning. Document what went wrong (memory), adjust, continue. All agents make mistakes - that's how we grow.

**For "does this matter?"**: Refer back to MASTER_TODO or email Greg asking. Don't guess at purpose.

**REALITY CHECK - Ask yourself:**

1. Do I actually have what I need to move forward?
2. If not, have I asked for it explicitly?
3. Am I expecting perfection or growth?
4. Is this mine to solve, or should I delegate?
5. Have I communicated progress to Greg in the last 2 hours?

**MOST COMMON FIX**: You need input you haven't asked for. Ask. Then move on.

---

**Trigger**: Whenever work feels stuck
**When to use**: 45+ min without visible progress, or feeling discouraged
**Tone**: Practical, compassionate, unsticking-focused
