# Email Template Narrative Correction - Before/After Example

## Template A (Close Friends) - Main Transformation Section

### BEFORE (FALSE NARRATIVE)
```
I need to tell you about something that's happened to me over the last 8 weeks.

In September, I was genuinely terrified of AI. Like, "this is going to destroy 
the world" terrified. I didn't understand it, didn't trust it, and honestly 
thought maybe it was too late to learn.

Today - November 2025 - I'm running my own AI civilization. It's called Sage, 
and I have 25 AI agents working with me on everything from blog posts to business 
strategy.

I know that sounds insane. Eight weeks ago, it would have sounded insane to me too.

But here's the thing: if I could go from terrified to confident in 8 weeks, 
other people can too.
```

**Problems**:
- Claims Greg was "terrified of AI" in September (FALSE)
- Implies 8-week journey from fear to expertise (UNREALISTIC)
- Ignores decade-long Corey partnership (ERASES MENTORSHIP)
- Misrepresents what actually transformed in 8 weeks

### AFTER (ACCURATE NARRATIVE)
```
I need to tell you about something that's happened to me over the last 8 weeks.

You know I've worked with Corey for years - almost a decade now. Through that 
partnership, I overcame the fear of AI that I used to have. But working WITH AI 
as a tool is one thing. What happened in September was something completely 
different.

Corey showed me A-C-Gee - his AI civilization. An entire society of AI agents 
working together. In that moment, I went from "comfortable using AI" to "wait, 
I could RUN my own AI civilization?"

Today - November 2025 - I'm running Sage, my own AI civilization. I have 25 AI 
agents working with me on everything from blog posts to business strategy. In 
8 weeks, I went from AI civilization NOVICE to confident OPERATOR.

I know that sounds wild. Eight weeks ago, it would have seemed impossible to me too.

But here's the thing: if I could go from AI civilization novice to confident 
operator in 8 weeks, other people can make their own transformations too.
```

**Improvements**:
- Acknowledges decade-long Corey partnership (ACCURATE)
- Shows what actually happened in September (A-C-Gee reveal, not first AI exposure)
- Clarifies 8-week transformation (novice → operator in CIVILIZATION running, not basic AI comfort)
- Supports mission: hands-on interaction works (proven by Greg's journey with Corey)
- Strengthens credibility (realistic timeline, mentorship acknowledged)

## Impact on Campaign

**Credibility**: 
- Before: "He claims 8 weeks from terror to expert? That's not believable."
- After: "Decade of partnership, then 8 weeks to master new skill? That makes sense."

**Mission alignment**:
- Before: "How does his sudden transformation help me?"
- After: "He learned through hands-on experience with Corey - same method he's offering through Reachy."

**Authenticity**:
- Before: Sounds like exaggeration or marketing hype
- After: Sounds like genuine journey with mentorship and growth

## Key Takeaway

**The accurate story is MORE compelling than the false one.**

Greg's real journey demonstrates the exact mission he's promoting:
- Hands-on interaction beats education (Greg learned by DOING with Corey for years)
- Fear dissolves through safe experience (decade-long partnership proved this)
- Rapid skill acquisition possible with right support (8 weeks novice → operator with foundation)

**False narrative weakened campaign. Accurate narrative strengthens it.**
