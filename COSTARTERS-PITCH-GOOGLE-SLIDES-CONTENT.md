## QUICK SETUP IN GOOGLE SLIDES:

1. Create new Google Slides presentation
2. Choose a dark theme or blank theme
3. For each slide:
   - Insert the image (full bleed or top 2/3 of slide)
   - Add text boxes with content above
   - Use consistent fonts (Roboto or similar sans-serif)
   - Large headings (36-44pt), body text (18-24pt)
4. Set slide transitions to "None" or "Fade" for smooth flow

**Total slides:** 6 (no fundraising slide per your directive)

**Presentation time:** ~5 minutes (1 minute per slide average)
