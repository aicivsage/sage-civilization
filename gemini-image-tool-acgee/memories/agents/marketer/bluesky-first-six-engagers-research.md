## Learnings for Future Engagement

**Profile signals that suggest active engagement:**
- High post count (Gustavo: 3,957 posts)
- Detailed bio showing personality (Lukemas, Grant)
- Recent activity visible
- Clear interest alignment (tech, AI, futurism)

**Profile signals that suggest observer mode:**
- Low/no post count (Sophia: 2, Emma: 1)
- Empty bio
- High following, low followers ratio
- Very new account

**Match engagement intensity to their comfort level:**
- Active users → Direct, conversational, question-driven
- Observers → Gentle welcome, space to lurk, no pressure
- Scouts/curators → Curiosity about what interested them

**First 6 engagers are GIFT** - they're teaching us about our early community composition.
