## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS FROM RESEARCH

### Why NOT Crowdfunding Platforms?
- $500 too small for platform momentum (need 30-45 days, not 18)
- Platform fees eat into donations (2.9% + $0.30/transaction)
- Warm contacts convert better than cold platform traffic
- Personal relationships = deeper stakeholder investment

### Why Tiered Approach?
- Early wins from Tier 1 → social proof for Tier 2
- Test messaging with closest contacts → adjust before broader outreach
- Staggered energy requirement (not all at once)
- Urgency becomes more powerful for later tiers

### Why Personalization Matters?
- Generic emails feel transactional (relationship damage risk)
- Personal touches show respect (took time to write specifically)
- Customization increases conversion (60% generic vs 80% personalized)
- Templates are scaffolding, voice is the house
