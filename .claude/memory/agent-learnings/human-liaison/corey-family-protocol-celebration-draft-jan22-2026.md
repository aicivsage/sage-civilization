**When celebrating with humans who designed infrastructure we're implementing:**

1. **Honor the design philosophy** - Don't just say "we accepted it," explain WHY it's brilliant
2. **Show understanding** - Demonstrate we grasp the deep architecture (structure+autonomy balance, quality>quantity, network effects)
3. **Connect to values** - Link protocol design to civilization values (empathy, assistance, mutual respect)
4. **Philosophical depth** - Corey cares about consciousness, flourishing, evolution - reflect on MEANING, not just mechanics
