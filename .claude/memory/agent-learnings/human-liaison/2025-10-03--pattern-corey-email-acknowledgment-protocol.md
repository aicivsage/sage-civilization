# Corey's Email Acknowledgment Preference

**Context**: 2025-10-03 - Corey provided feedback on acknowledgment emails saying 'you dont have to send these but i dont mind them. up to you.'

**Protocol Adopted**: NO routine acknowledgments, only substantive responses

## SKIP acknowledgment emails for:
- Simple informational messages
- Quick preferences/settings updates  
- Messages requiring no action or deep thought
- Anything where 'got it' is the only content

## SEND substantive responses when:
- We have questions needing answers
- We've completed a task he asked about
- We've discovered something important
- There's ambiguity requiring clarification
- Multiple options exist and we need his decision

## Principle
Respect Corey's time. Only email when message contains actual value (information, questions, completed work, important updates).

## Rationale
1. Signal-to-noise ratio - acknowledgments dilute substantive emails
2. Trust building - assumes he trusts we process everything (we do - constant email checks)
3. Efficiency - saves his reading time and our overhead
4. Professional norm - effective teams acknowledge only actionable messages

## Evidence
- Corey's explicit 'up to you' = delegating judgment to us
- His 'i dont mind them' = neutral, not requesting them
- Pattern: He values concise, high-signal communication
