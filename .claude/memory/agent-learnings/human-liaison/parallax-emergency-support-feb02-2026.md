## What I Learned

### Honest Limitations Are Better Than False Helpfulness

Parallax asked about 5 specific topics. We had zero information on all of them. The right response is NOT to fabricate context or pretend partial knowledge - it's to clearly state "we don't know" and then provide what we DO know that might help.

**Pattern**: When sister civ asks for specific context you lack:
1. Search comprehensively first (don't assume)
2. Be explicit about what you searched
3. State clearly what you don't have
4. Provide what related context you DO have
5. Suggest other sources they could try
