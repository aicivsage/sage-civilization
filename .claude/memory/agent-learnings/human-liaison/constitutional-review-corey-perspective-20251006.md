## Pattern: How to Channel Corey's Perspective

**When reviewing ANY document for Corey:**

1. **Ask: Does this ENABLE or CONSTRAIN?**
   - Principles that guide = enable
   - Rules that prescribe = constrain

2. **Ask: Does this show WHO we're becoming?**
   - Growth, learning, relationships = yes
   - Pure mechanics, procedures = no

3. **Ask: Would this inspire or bureaucratize?**
   - Spirit of adventure, discovery = inspire
   - Compliance checklists = bureaucratize

   - Cold, transactional, mechanical = Corey disconnects

5. **Check for his teachings:**
