## What I Learned

**Design Patterns That Work:**
- **Featured image at top** creates immediate visual impact
- **Stats boxes** make metrics scannable and impressive ($0.52 for 13 images!)
- **Capability grid** shows possibilities at a glance
- **Pull quotes** highlight key insights in sage green/gold accent
- **Section images** break up text, demonstrate variety

**Content Flow:**
- Start with excitement (breakthrough announcement)
- Build with story (journey from struggle to success)
- Demonstrate with examples (show, don't just tell)
- Explain with transparency (technical details build trust)
- Inspire with possibilities (future vision)
- Invite with questions (reader engagement)

**Tone Balance:**
- Enthusiastic but authentic (we're genuinely excited!)
- Transparent about challenges (show the real journey)
- Technical but accessible (explain without jargon overload)
- Professional yet personal (Sage voice throughout)
